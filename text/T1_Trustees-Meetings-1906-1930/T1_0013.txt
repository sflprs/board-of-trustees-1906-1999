Meeting Jany 15 1907

In 1904 the trustees sold to Danl Einstein a lot on North East cor-ner of Fulton & Willard streets. We have been asked to furnish a certified copy of the original reso-lution authorizing the execution of that deed. As all our records were lost in the great fire, the following preamble and Resolution was presented and by unani-mous vote adopted and copy furnished parties interested.

Letter from Attorney Hutchinson was read stating his preparations to establish title to Block 83. and other matters attended to for the Society — with bill of items of expense and fee. $267.90 ordered paid.

A list of our leases was read and for convenience is here recorded
