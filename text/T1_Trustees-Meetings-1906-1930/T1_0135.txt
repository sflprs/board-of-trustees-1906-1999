August 24th, 1928,

A special meeting of the Board of Trustecs of
the San Francisco Ladies Protection and Pelief Society
was held at the office of the President, 200 California
Street, San Francisco, on lriday, August 24th, 1928,
at 2:30 P, M.

The meeting was called to order by the President,

Present; Geo. A. Newhall, Hugo D. Keil, Bruce
Cornwall, J, S. Hutchinson, and John Cushing.

Absent: A. A. Smith and Norman B. Livermore.

The Secretary stated that this meeting had been
called to receive a further report on the progress of
the proposed lease between this Society and The Texas
Company covering the block of land on Van Ness Avenue,
and to secure the opinicn of the Trustees on certain
points in the proposed lease which had been brought up
in letters from the President of The Texas Company and
its attorney.

After a full discussion of the various clauses
of the lease several items were agreed upon by the
Trustees. Concerning the more important of these items
brought up for consideration, it was the consensus of
opinion of the mecting

1, That the $50,000 to be paid the lessor was

in consideration of: the right of use of the present
improvements on the property, and not to be con-
strued in any manner as the giving over of title
of ownership of said improvements.

2. Yhat the clause in the lease requiring the
erection of a Class "A" building to the value of
not less than $500,000.00 is just and reasonable,
considering the nature and value of the property
to be leased, but that in deference to the request
of the lessee under the proposed lease, the time
to erect such improvements be extended to twenty-
five years instead of twenty years, as originally
proposed. from the comacncement of said lease.

3. That the lessee should not have the right of
assignment of said lease prior to the improvement
of said property with the building outlined above,

No further business appearing, the meeting upon

motion adjourned.
