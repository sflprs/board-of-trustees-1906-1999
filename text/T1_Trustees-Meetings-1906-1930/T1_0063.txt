A special meeting of the Board of Trustees of the San Francisco Ladies Protection & Relief Society was held at the office of the President, Mr Geo A Newhall, on the 17th day of February 1916, at 2.30 P.M. for the purpose of considering the leasing of the Napa property belonging to the said San Francisco Ladies Protection & Relief Society.

There were present. trustees Newhall, Keil & Lovell absent Davis and Hutchinson.

President Newhall occupied the chair and called the meeting to order.

The Secretary then read the minutes of the last meeting which on motion duly seconded were ordered approved as read.

After duly discussing the matter the following resolution was introduced, and after being duly seconded, was unanimously carried;
