Clause No. 12. The lessee shall keep the buildings now standing on
said demised premises in substantially as good repair as they now are in
up to and until the time when lessee shall determine to remove and shall
remove the same for the erection of new or other buildings as herein pro-
vided for and also shall keep said buildings insured at its own expense, but
for the benefit of the lessor, and will likewise keep any other building or
buildings hereafter constructed on the demised land insured against loss
or damage by fire for at least seventy-five (75) per cent of their insur-
able value, exclusive of foundations. All of such insurance shall be
placed with responsible insurance companies legally authorized to trans-
act business in the State of California and maintaining offices or agen-
cies in the City and County of San Francisco. All such insurance policies
and renewals thereof shall be deposited with the American Trust Com-
pany of San Francisco, or such other trust company located in said City
and County of San Francisco as may from time to time be designated in
writing by the lessor herein, to act as Trustee, and any loss thereunder
shall be made payable to said trust company as Trustee, to the end that
said Trustee shall be entitled to collect for the use and benefit of lessor
and lessee all money due under said policies payable in the event of and
by reason of the loss or damage by fire of the building or buildings situ-
ated on said land. So far as the same may be reasonably procurable from
such insurance companies without increase in rate, said policies shall be
so conditioned, either upon the face of the policies or by standard form
of loss clause, that they shall provide that any loss shall be payable to said
Trustee for the benefit of the lessor, notwithstanding any act or negli-
gence of lessee which might otherwise result in a forfeiture of said insur-
ance.

All insurance money received by said Trustee shall be held by it in
trust; first, as additional security for the payment to lessor of the rentals
and other charges herein provided for, pending the rebuilding or repair-
ing of the building or buildings damaged or destroyed; and, secondly, in
trust for the purpose of defraying the cost of rebuilding or repairing such
building or buildings, as hereinbefore provided; and, finally, for the pur-
pose of paying to lessee any excess remaining in the hands of the Trustee
after the work of rebuilding or repairing, as the case may be, shall have
been concluded and fully paid for. That is to say:
