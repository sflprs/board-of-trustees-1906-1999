April 15th 1910

A special meeting of the Board of Trustees, due notice of which was served upon each was held at office of President Newhall at 2. P.M this day

Present trustees Newhall Bishop. Keil and Hutchinson a quorum.

Minutes of meeting of March 14th last. were approved as read

President Newhall stated that he had called the meeting to present a letter from Baldwin & Howell real estate agents, in reply to his enquiry about terms for long time leases.

Letter was read discussed. and ordered filed. It suggested a 4% rate upon sliding scale of valuation. $200.000 the first five years yielding $8000 pr annum. for the last ten years of a thirty year lease $350.000 as the valuation yeilding $14000 pr annum. They do not advise a longer lease than thirty years.
