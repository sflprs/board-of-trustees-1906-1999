October 19, 1928.

A special meeting of the Board of Trustees of the
San Francisco Ladies’ Protection and Relief Society was
held at the office of the President, 260 California
Street, San Francisco, on Friday, October 19, 1928, at
3:00 P. KH.

The meeting was called to order by the President.

Present: Geo. A. Newhall, Norman B. Livermore,
J. S. Hutchinson and Bruce Cornwall.

Absent: Hugo D. Keil, John E. Cushing, and Arthur A.
Smith.

The minutes of the meetings of Trustees held on
May 11, July 24, and August 24, 1928, were read and
approved as recorded.

On motion of Hr. Bruce Cornwall, seconded by
Mr. Norman B. Livermore, the following resolution was
unanimously adopted:

RESOLVED by the Directors of the San Francisco
Ladies’ Protection and Relief Society that this
corporation lease to The Texas Company, for the
term of ninety-nine years, irom and after the Ist
day of October, 1928, all of that certain real
property situated in the City and County of San
Francisco and described as follows:

All that certain block of land known as Western
Addition Block No. Bichty-three (83) and which
said block is bounded on the North by the
Southerly line of Post Street, on the West by
the Easterly line of Franklin Street, on the
South by the Northerly line of Geary Street,
and on the East by the Westerly line of Van
Ness Avenue, and which said block further has

a frontage on Post Street of Three hundred and
Bighty-four (384) feet and nine (9) inches and
on Franklin Street of Two hundred and Seventy-
five (275) feet, and on Geary Street of Three
hundred and Lighty-four (384) feet and Nine (9)
inches, and on Van Ness Avenue of Two hundred
and Seventy-five (275) feet, and is one solid
rectancular contiguous piece of property with-
out any diminuation by reason of any street,
alley or easenent;

And that this corporation enter into the lease of
said premises in the words and figures hereinafter
set forth, and that the President and Secretary

of this corporation be and they are hereby author-
ized and directed, in the name, under the seal and
on behalf of this corporation, to make, execute
and deliver an instrument in writing, leasing the
said premises to the said The Texas Company for
the term of ninety-nine years, upon the terms and
conditions hereinafter set forth.
