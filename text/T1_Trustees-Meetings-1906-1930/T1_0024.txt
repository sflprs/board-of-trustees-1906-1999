Sept 7th 1909.

A special meeting of the Board was held at the "Home" this day at 11 O'clk A.M.

Present. Trustees Newhall Bishop. Kiel & Hutchinson a quorum

The lady managers were holding their regular monthly meeting at the time and we invited to meet with them

The discussion was upon the question of clearing off the cheap constructions put upon our property by the lessees three years since, time now expired.

Mr Newhall suggested that Van Ness Ave and Geary street frontages be entirely cleared of buildings, the lots fenced, and of-fered for long lease

An agent of Baldwin & Howell was present, and spoke of the dormant condition of property on the Avenue, was doubtful about finding lessees at present, either for long or short terms, even if the ground was cleared.

Mr Kiel and Mr Bishop thought the few tenants remaining in the stores, who are paying in all, over $300 monthly might remain from month to month untill something
