He mentioned a client who would bid $750 per month for our Van Ness frontage by 125 feet in depth for a 50 year lease. He thought perhaps the bid might be increased a little. but it did not interest the trustees.

Trustee Kiel said a party had spoken to him about a 50 year lease of one half the block upon the basis of 5% rental upon an appraisal of its value made after each period of ten years. The proposal was in contemplation of build- -ing a fine first class hotel. It was voted that Mr Kiel invite the party to call upon President Newhall to talk over the matter.

It was deemed desirable to place the condition of our finances before the Managers, and by vote the secretary was instructed to ask Miss Beaver to request the Secretary and the treasurer to join her in a conference with trustees at the Home at 10. O'clk Tuesday June 4th
