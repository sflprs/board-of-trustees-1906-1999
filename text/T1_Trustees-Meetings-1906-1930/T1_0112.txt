October 29, 1925.

A special meeting of the Board of Trustees of the San
Francisco Ladies Protection and Relief Society was held at
he office of the President, Newhall Building, 260 Califor-
nia Street, San Francisco, California, on Thursday, Uctober
29th 1925 at 11 a.m.

Present: Geo. A. Newhall, Norman B, Livermore and Bruce
Cornwall,

Absent. Hugo D. Keil and Arthur A, Smith,

Upon motion duly made and seconded the following
resolution was unanimously adopted;

RESOLVED: That the action of the Board of Trustees in
agreeing to lease to the California Petroleum Corporation the
unleased portion of the property belonging to the Society at
Van Ness Avenue and Franklin Streets, between Post and Geary
Streets, San Francisco, be confirmed and that the attorney
for the Society be instructed and authorized to draw such a
lease upon the following general terms;

The term to be eighteen years from December 10, 1925,
with the total rental of $137,700, payable as follows:

. $525.00 monthly from December 10, 1925 to December 2, 1928.
