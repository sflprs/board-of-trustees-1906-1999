May 23d 1912 A special meeting of the Board of Trustees was held at the office of President Newhall at 11. O'clk. Present Newhall. Davis Kiel and Hutchinson.

President stated that he called the meeting to consider the financial condition of the society. He also stated that he had attended a meeting [struck through: a meeting] of the Board of Health with reference to their notice about the shacks on [struck through: our] Van Ness Ave frontage and had signified to them our intention to clear off the property, obtaining from them permission to delay until further notice.

Treasurer was asked as to our cash balance in bank and stated that it was reduced to $1824.— by calls from the Managers since February of $600 $2000. $1000. $1500 and payment of taxes $2329.11.

How shall we provide funds for the institution was then discus- -sed at great lenght.

President Newhall said he had thought of a sale of our block. investing proceeds in acres and a suitable building for our wants He had summoned an agent of Baldwin & Howell to be present. so as to enquire as to the values of Van Ness property.
