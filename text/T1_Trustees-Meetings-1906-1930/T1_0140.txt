Clause 1. THIS INDENTURE OF LEASE made and entered into
this Ist day of October, 1928, by and between DIRECTORS OF SAN
FRANCISCO LADIES’ PROTECTION AND RELIEF SOCIETY, a
corporation incorporated and existing under and by virtue of the laws of
the State of California, and having its office and principal place of busi-
ness at the City and County of San Francisco, in said State, the party of
the first part, hereinafter designated as lessor, and THE TEXAS COM-
PANY, a corporation incorporated and existing under and by virtue of
the laws of the State of California, and having its office and principal
place of business in the City of Los Angeles, County of Los Angeles, State
aforesaid, party of the second part, hereinafter designated as lessee;

WITNESSETH:

Clause 2. That the lessor, in consideration of the rents, covenants and
agreements hereinafter contained to be paid, kept and performed by the
lessee and upon the condition that each and all of the said covenants and
agreements shall be fully kept and performed by the lessee, does by these
presents demise and let unto the lessee and the lessee does hereby hire and
take from the lessor those certain premises situate in the City and County
of San Francisco, State of California, and more particularly described as
follows, to-wit:

Clause 3. All of that certain block of land known as Western Addi-
tion Block No. Eighty-three (83) and which said block is bounded on the
north by the southerly line of Post Street, on the west by the easterly line of
Franklin Street, on the south by the northerly line of Geary Street and on
the east by the westerly line of Van Ness Avenue, and which said block fur-
ther has a frontage on Post Street of Three Hundred Eighty-four (384)
feet nine(9) inches and on Franklin Street of Two Hundred Seventy-five
(275) feet and on Geary Street of Three Hundred Eighty-four (384) feet
nine (9) inches and on Van Ness Avenue of Two Hundred Seventy-five
(275) feet, and is one solid rectangular contiguous piece of property with-
out any diminution by reason of any street, alley or easement.

Together with the buildings and improvements thereon and with all
rights, privileges and easements thereunto appertaining or belonging.
Subject, however, to the existing leases hereinafter specifically referred
to. The lessor does not let or demise unto the lessee any space under, in
or upon the street or sidewalk adjacent to the property hereby demised,
but the lessor does let and demise to the lessee during the continuance of
the term of this lease and subject to all of the terms and conditions hereof
such rights as the lessor may have to use any such space—subject, how-
ever, to the public power of license, regulation or termination, without
affecting this lease, and the lessee shall pay any license fee or other charge
imposed or demanded by any lawful authority in respect thereto.
