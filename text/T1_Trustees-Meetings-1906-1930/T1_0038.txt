Mch 25th 1913.

At call of Prest Newhall the trustees met at his office at 11. O'Clk Present. Bishop. Davis. Keil Newhall Hutchinson the full Board.

Prest stated that it had been sug- -gested to him that the Hecht house on Post St opposite our property was to be torn down. that it might be bought for $500 and moved on to our Geary St front. for $1300. On motion of trustee Kiel it was voted that we do not wish to buy the house.

The matter of lease to Helbush now came up for discussion.

Mr Helbush came in with his Atty and the 30 day option and lease drawn up by Atty Hutchinson was read by him and commented upon seriatum. With few minor changes the lease was finally agreed upon. and the option of 30 days signed by both parties. For the Society Geo A Newhall Charles R Bishop Horace Davis Hugo D Kiel and J. S. Hutchinson.

By the lessor H H Helbush & Co. who deposited check for $400. the money to be returned when the lease is signed.
