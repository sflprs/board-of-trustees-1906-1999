A meeting of trustees, adjourned from May 23d was held at the "Home" on Tuesday June 4th at 10. O'clk

Present trustees Newhall, Davis Bishop, Kiel and Hutchinson

The officers of the lady managers having been invited to meet with trustees in conference upon the finan- -cial condition of the society. Miss Beaver and Mrs Austin [?]. President and Secretary were present.

For the information of the ladies Prest Newhall stated the proposals to lease the Van Ness Ave frontage that have been received. for terms of 20 and 50 years. Also: he stated the need of increase of income or decrease of expenses as our bank account runs very low.

Miss Beaver thought that the expenses of the institution could not be reduced. explaining. the requirements of up to date methods under the law.

After a long discussion as to leasing for a term of years with privilege of purchase of the whole block. it was decided to ask from the proposing lessee his scheme in writing.

If obtained. Mr Newhall to call another conference with managers..

Adjourned
                    J. S. Hutchinson
                         Secy
