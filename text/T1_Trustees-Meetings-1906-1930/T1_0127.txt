October 28th, 1927,

A meeting of the Board of Trustecs ot the
San Francisco Ladies Protection and Relief Society was
held in the office of the President, Newhall Building,
260 California Street, San francisco, on October 28th,
1927, at 2:30 P. M.

The mceting was called to order by the
President.

Present: Geo. A, Newhall, Norman 8, Livermore, Bruce
Cornwall, J. S,. Hutchinson, John Cushing.

Absent: Humwo D. Keil and A. A, Smith.

The minutes of the meeting held on April 28th,
1927, were read and approved as recorded.

Upon motion duly made and seconded, the
followine resolutions were unaninously adopted:

1. PESOLVED: That in the matter of the purchase
of a burial plot for the Society, it was decided to refer
this question to the President [or action after he had
procured an expression of opinion from the Lady Nanagers,
and that he discuss same with the Masonic and Cypress
Lawn Cemeteries.

2. PRESCLVED: That the request of the Pacific Gas
& Flectric Company for a rieht-of-way for a pole line
across the Camp Smith property be granted and that the
document be completed by the proper officers on the
recomuendation of the attorney in due course.

The attorney announced that Mrs. Bellisle who
now has a lease on the property on Post Street desired to
assign her lease to one named Baker. After some dis-
cussion it was agreed to leave the matter in the attorney's
hands for action with the recommendation that it would be
better if possible to make a new lease with Mr. Baker for
say ten years at $200.00 a month, and failine this on as
advantaweous terms as possible,

No further business appearing, the meeting

upon motion adjourned,
