[Book page 28]

April 28th 1911. a special meeting of the Board of Trustees was held at 2. O'Clk P.M. this day, at the office of trustee Bishop

Present trustees Bishop, Kiel and Hutchinson. a quorum.

Trustee Bishop presiding.

Secretary stated that the call was to consider a proposal which he read: from the Fleischackers for the deposit of one hundred shares of City Electric stock we hold, with the Anglo-Paris American Bank. pending negotiations for sale of the stock at $80 per share. This was considered favorably and upon motion of trustee Kiel it was voted that the Secretary, deposit the certificate with the Bank taking its receipt.

Trustee Hutchinson stated that attorney for David Einstein has been making enquiries to know if a compromise of our suit against him can be made. When asked the amount he would offer $500. was named. This is less than 10 prct of the amount due us under his lease. Such a sum would not be considered, trustee Bishop sugges- that we might consider a 50 prct offer and it was voted to notify Einstein's attorney verbally to this effect.

Adjourned
                    J. S. Hutchinson
                         Secy
