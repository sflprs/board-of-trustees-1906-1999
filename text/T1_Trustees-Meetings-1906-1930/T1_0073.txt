Mr. Lovell further stated that Mr. Helbush, on behalf
of the Van Ness Realty Company, was desirous of having the
present lease of the Van Ness Avenue frontage extended for a
period of three years and eight months, -- that is, to and
including the 5th day of January, 1943, -- at the monthly

‘ rental of $1,310.00 per month, payable in advance, during the
term of said extension, and had offered to pay an additional
$50.00 per month for twelve years, commencing January 6, 1917,
for the privilege of such extension.

On motion duly made and seconded, it was Resolved that
the President and Secretary of this corporation be, and they
are hereby, given authority to extend for the term of three
years and eight months, -- that is, to and including the 5th
day of January, 1943, -- that certain lease dated May 6, 1913,
given by this Society to Van Ness Realty Company.

There being no further business to come before the meeting,
on motion duly seconded, it was declared adjourned, subject to the

call of the chair.
