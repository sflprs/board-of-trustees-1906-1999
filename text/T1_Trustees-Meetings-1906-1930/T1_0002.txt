May 23, 1906

All records and papers of the Trustees of "San Francisco Ladies Protection & Relief Society" from its organ-ization, were burned in the great fire of April 18th 19th & 20th 1906.

The minutes of the Secretary of the lady Managers record, that at the annual meet-ing of the Society held January 1906, the following named were elected trustees.
    George A Newhall
    Charles R Bishop
    James S Hutchinson
    Horace Davis
    Hugo Keil.

At a meeting of the trustees held in February 1906 the Board Organized by electing
    Trustee. Geo A Newhall Prest.
       "    J. S. Hutchinson Secy & Treas.
