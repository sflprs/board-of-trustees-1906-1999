Meeting of May 23. 1906

to pay tax bill, Parent's bill for repairs, and for needed books and stationery, it was so voted.

On motion of trustee Keil it was voted that Jos Hutchinson Atty of the Board be requested to procure a copy of the Horace Hawes deed; and from an au-thenticated copy to furnish each trustee with a copy, also; with his opinion, if under terms of the deed we are at liberty to locate the "Home" outside the City of San Francisco.

Our attorney also: to procure copy of lease of the Keely property Napa Co. and necessary steps to protect all our rights.

Baldwin & Howell the real estate agents who have had the renting of our houses for years past, reported to the trustees several bids made for a two year lease of the entire frontage between Post and Geary, 275 feet on Van Ness Avenue by 100 feet in depth. $1500 [?] $2100. and $2500. payable monthly in advance. The bid of $2500 was by Freidlander, Goldstone Company. After investigation as to the plans, and as to responsibility of this concern, which agreed that Baldwin & Howell should collect all rents of the tenants and apply same first in payments to us in terms of a lease, such a lease was prepared.
