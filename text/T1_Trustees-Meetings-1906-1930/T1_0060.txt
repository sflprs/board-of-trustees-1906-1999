[Book page 58]

A meeting of the Board of Trustees. of the San Francisco Ladies Protection and Relief Society was held at the office of Wm Newhall & Co at 2 P.M. on the 24th [?] day of January 1916 for the purpose of organizing.

There were present directors. Hutchinson Newhall. Keil and Lovell. absent director Davis.

Mr Keil occupied the chair as temporary chairman.

The meeting being called to order by the Chair. Director Hutchinson nominated Mr Geo A Newhall for President of the Board and on being seconded by director Keil on vote Mr Newhall was elected President to serve for the ensuing term, and until his successor is elected.

Director Keil then nominated Mr Mansfield Lovell as Secretary and Treasurer of the Board and on being seconded by director Hutchinson on vote Mr Lovell was elected Secretary and Treasurer. to serve for the ensuing term and until his successor is elected.

The [?] minutes of the previous meeting of 30th July 1914 were then read, and on motion duly seconded, were approved as read.

Director Hutchinson then offered the following resolution.

That Geo. A Newhall, President and Mansfield Lovell Secretary jointly, of this corporation be and they are hereby authorized to sign checks and drafts for and on behalf of this corporation.
