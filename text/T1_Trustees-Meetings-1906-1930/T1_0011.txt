[Typed insert:]

Whereas, the Board of Trustees of the San Francisco Ladies' Protection and Relief Society, a beneficial society, a cor-poration organized and existing under and by virtue of the laws of the State of California, did authorize the proper officers of said Society, to wit: the President and Secretary of the Board of Trustees, to execute that certain deed of said Society to Daniel Einstein of that certain property at the Northeast corner of Fulton and Willard Streets, in the City and County of San Francisco, State of California, dated December 22, 1904, recorded December 29, 1904, in Liber 2102 of Deeds, page 35, per the County Recorder's office of the City and County of San Francisco, State of California, and to which said records reference is hereby had for a more definite description of said property; and, whereas, the records of said Society have been destroyed, so that a certified copy of the original resolution authorizing the execution of said deed cannot now be fur-nished, --

Now, Therefore, be it RESOLVED that, at a regular meeting of the Board of Trustees of said Society held this 15th day of January, A. D. 1907, said Board of Trustees, by unanimous con-sent, do hereby ratify, confirm and re-affirm the execution of the above-mentioned deed, and the President and Secretary are hereby authorized and directed, for and on behalf of and in the name of this corporation, to properly execute a copy of this resolution and deliver same for record.
