Augt. 11th 1913. Trustee Chas R Bishop handed me a bond of the Pacific Union Club $1000. stating that he wished to anticipate an item in his will made four years ago, in which he bequeathed to the S F Ladies Protection & Relief Society One Thousand dollars

He had prepared a receipt setting forth that this bond was accepted in lieu of the bequest named in his will. This receipt I signed as treasurer of Board of Trustees. placed the bond in Safe Deposit Box of Trustees and notified Miss Beaver Prest of Managers. that they should duly acknowledge the gift to Mr Bishop.
                        J. S. Hutchinson
                             Treas
