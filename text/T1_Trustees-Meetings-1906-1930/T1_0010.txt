Meeting of January 15 1907.

Pursuant to notice of President Newhall mailed to each trustee, a meeting of the Board of Trustees was held this day at 11. O'Clk A.M. at office of H. M. Newhall & Co. A quorum present.

Minutes of meeting of Oct 26. 1906 were read and ap-proved.

Secretary reported the delivery of lease to L Kriess & Sons of 9 ft 4 in frontage on Post St by a depth of 89 feet. Also: the renewal of the lease of the Napa Co. (Head [?] at $250 per annum). This item concludes the minutes of the Board for 1906.

At the annual meeting of the San Francisco Ladies Protect-ion & Relief Society held January 10th 1907. Geo A Newhall. Charles R Bishop. Horace Davis. Hugo Keil and Jas S. Hutchinson were duly elected trustees for the ensuing year. The members [struck through: of the] being same as last year, The Board proceeded to organ-ize by electing

    Geo A Newhall President
    J. S. Hutchinson Secy & Treasr.
