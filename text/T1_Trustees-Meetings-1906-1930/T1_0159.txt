Clause No. 22. Lessor and lessee mutually covenant that should there
arise from time to time during this lease term any situation or condition
not contemplated and provided for in other paragraphs of this lease, and
which situation or condition affects injuriously the rights and interests
of either or both of the parties hereto, the lessor and lessee will annoint
arbitrators in the number and by the method and of the qualifications as
heretofore provided, for the appointment of rental appraisers, and such
arbitrators shall have full power to decide and determine the rights,
duties and obligations of the parties hereto under the terms of this lease,
and such decision and determination shall be binding on the parties hereto.
