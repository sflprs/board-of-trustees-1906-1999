May 5th 1913

At call of Prest Newhall a meeting of trustees of S. F Ladies Protection and Relief Society. was held at his office at 3. P.M

Present trustees Newhall. Bishop. Kiel. and Hutchinson.

The draft of a lease of the Van Ness Avenue lot. to the Van Ness Realty Co was read by attorney Hutchinson and discussed. deemed satisfactory and trustee Kiel offered the follow- -ing resolution. which was voted unanimously:
