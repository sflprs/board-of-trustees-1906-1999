Mr. Lovell further stated that Mr. Helbush had made a
proposition on behalf of the Van Ness Realty Company to lease
from the Society the Geary Street lot, 100 feet by 100 feet,
for $75.00 per month and taxes, for a period of twenty-six years
that he would erect a Class "A", "B" or "C" building on the F
premises, to cost not less than $12,000.00; that he would pay i
four months' rent in advance, upon the execution of the lease,
and should then have the remainder of the first year, -- that
is, eight months, -- free from all rent; that Mr. Helbush had
further stated that he would pay $50.00 per month for twelve
years, for an extension of the lease of May 6, 1913, and that
said $50.00 per month might be added to the payments called for
by the said new lease, for a period of twelve years.

On motion duly made and seconded, it was unanimously Re-
solved that this corporation lease to Van Ness Realty Company ,
(a corporation), a lot on the North line of Geary Street,
157 1/2 feet West of Van Ness Avenue, with a frontage of 100
feet on said Geary Street and a depth Northerly of 100 feet,
for the term of twenty-six (26) years, from January 6 1917,
to and including January 5, 1943, at the total rental of Thirty
thousand (30,000) Dollars, payable Three hundred (300) Dollars
upon the execution of said lease, Fifty (50) Dollars per
month during the first year, One hundred and Twenty-five (125)
Dollars per month during the next eleven years, and Seventy-
five (75) Dollars per month during the fourteen years there-
after, -- aid lease to contain the condition that the lessee
erect upon said premises, within one year from the date of said
lease, a Class "A", "B" or "C" building at a cost of not less
than Twelve thousand (12,000) Dollars, and also that the lessee
pay all the taxes, commencing with the year 1917-18.
