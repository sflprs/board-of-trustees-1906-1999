Meeting Sept 19. 1907

necessary by expiry of the orig-inal charter.

Upon motion of trustee Bishop it was voted that the President and Secretary of this Board be duly authorized to sign papers assenting to the change of name and transfer of our funds to The Savings and Loan Society.

Treasurer reported that under resolu-tion authorizing Presdt. and Treasr to invest funds on deposit. they bought

    on Apl 27 $10000. U Ry Bonds @ 75.50 [?]
    Apl 30 $10.000 1st Funding S. Pac 94.

A communication from Baldwin & Howell addressed to Mrs Willard treasurer of Lady Managers was read. It stated that tenant Wheeler lessee of house 1217. Post street was asking a reduction of rent, No action was had by the board, his lease expires June 1909

Discussion was had in regard to investing money in Saving Bank in bonds or other securities. Bank of California shares were named and by vote trustees Newhall and Bishop were requested to ascertain, if shares are offered, and to buy ten or twenty if price is regular.. Adjourned.

                        J. S. Hutchinson Secy
