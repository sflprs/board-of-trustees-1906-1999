[In margin: June 19 1906]

All our securities contained in Safe Deposit Vault were found in order, when weeks after the fire the debris of the building had cooled and was cleared away permitting access to the box.

A general discussion of the affairs of the Society now took place. The pressing need of providing a new "Home" where our wards may be brought together again was urged. The children and their attendants are now provided for in several sep-arate groups, since our old "Home" was vacated on the 18th of April.

It was stated that enquiries were being made for a suitable location and acre property within this county.

In the destruction of the Hall of Records in this city, the record of the Horace Hawes deed was lost. The Managers by a vote expressed their desire that the Trustees take immediate steps to establish our title to Block No 83.

The Trustees now withdrew and held a special meeting pursuant to call of President issued on 15th inst. Present. Trustees Newhall, Bishop, Keil and Hutchinson.
