Meeting of Feby 6. 1908 continued.

At the Annual Meeting of the San Francisco Ladies Protection & Relief Society held at the "Home" January 9. 1908. Geo A Newhall Charles R Bishop. Horace Davis. Hugo D Keil and J. S. Hutchinson were elected trustees for the en-suing year.

The board now proceed-ed to organize for 1908. by electing

    Trustee Geo. A Newhall. President
       "    J S Hutchinson. Secy & Treasr

without further business adjourned

                        J. S. Hutchinson Secy
