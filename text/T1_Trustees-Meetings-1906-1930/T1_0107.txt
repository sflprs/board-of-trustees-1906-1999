June 4th, 1925.

The regular monthly meeting of the Board of
trustees of the San Francisco Ladies Protection and
Relief Society was held at the office of the President,
Newhall Building, 260 California Street, San Francisco,
California, on Thursday, June 4th 1925 at 11 a.m.

Present, Geo. A. Newhall, Bruce Cornwall and Norman
B, Livermore. Also Atty. J.S. Hutchinson,

Absent. Hugo D. Keil and Arthur A. Smith.

The minutes of the meetings held on December s
15th 1924 and January 12th 1925 were read and approved
as recorded,

Upon motion duly made and seconded the following
resolutions were unanimously adopted:

1. RESOLVED: That the action of the President or
Vice President and Secretary, in accepting and making
payment of the following architect's Certificates,
signed by Julia Morgan, and approved by the Board of
Lady Managers Building Committee, payable to D.B.
Farquharson on account of contract for the new building
at Bay and Laguna Streets, San Francisco, be ratified:
