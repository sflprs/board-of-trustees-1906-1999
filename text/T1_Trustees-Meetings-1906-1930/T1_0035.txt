July 31st 1912

At call of Prest Newhall a meeting of the trustees was held at his office at 10.30 A.M

Present. Bishop, Davis. Newhall Hutchinson a quorum

President stated that notice had come from the Board of Health that the stores on our Van Ness avenue frontage must be vacated at once.

Baldwin & Howell were directed to notify tenants to leave by Augt 15th

Several proposals to lease the prop- -erty were presented. One that we should build covering ground as per plans and receive 4% on cost and value of lot. Philip Dibert [?] offered $850 per month for 30 year lease with option of 20 years more rent to begin July 1. 1913

Niether of these proposals would meet our needs. Baldwin & Howell submitted a proposal of a client of theirs for a twenty or twenty five year lease at $1000 per month. for 275 x 137 ft. they to pay taxes and any assessments. This proposal was discussed at length. as also our urgent need of funds to meet current expenses of the Managers.

The trustees were inclined to favor this last proposal, and voted, that the Prest obtain specific detail of their plans. also: how they would secure us. Adjourned to meet Aug. 7th at 10.30 A.M
                    J. S. Hutchinson
                         Secy
