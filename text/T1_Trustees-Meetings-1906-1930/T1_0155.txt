Clause No. 16. The lessor, its agents and representatives shall at all
reasonable times have the right to go upon the demised premises and all
parts thereof and to post and keep posted thereon a notice or notices to
the effect that said lessor and its property will not be liable by reason of
any materials furnished or work performed on the demised premises, and
the lessee promises and agrees not to take down or disturb said notices or
to do anything which may cause said notices or any of them to lack the
character of legal notice. Lessor and its agents may likewise enter said
premises for any other reasonable purposes incidental to the rights of lessor.

Clause No. 17. The failure, neglect or omission of lessor to terminate
this lease for any one or more breaches of any of the covenants hereof
shall not stop, bar or prevent it from terminating this lease for any sub-
sequent breach of any such covenant, or for any other or prior breach
that has not been consented to by it. The acceptance of rent by lessor
shall not at any time be deemed a waiver of any preceding breach by
lessee, or a consent by it to such breach.

Clause No. 18. It is further agreed that in case any improvement and
building on said premises shall be at any time damaged or destroyed by
fire, earthquake or other casualty, this lease shall not thereby be termi-
nated even though all said buildings be so destroyed, the laws of the State
of California to the contrary notwithstanding.

Clause No. 19. It is further covenanted and agreed by any between
the parties hereto that any demand for rent made after it becomes due
shall have the same force and effect as if made on the day it falls due;
that if any default shall be made by the lessee in any payment of rents
or taxes, assessments, insurance premiums, water or other rates, or any
other sum herein stipulated and agreed to be paid or kept, or the said
lessee shall fail to keep and perform any other covenant, condition or
agreement herein provided, on the part of the lessee to be performed, and
such default shall exist for a period of sixty (60) days, then and in that
case, lessor may serve upon lessee written notice of such default; and if
