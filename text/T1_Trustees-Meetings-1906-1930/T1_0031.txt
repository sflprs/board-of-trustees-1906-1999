[Book page 29]

Pursuant to notice a meeting of the Trustees was held at the office of President Newhall January 16th 1912

Present Bishop, Davis, Newhall and Hutchinson. absent Keil

At the annual meeting Jany 12 all the members were re-elected. [struck through: and] Upon nomination of trustee Davis. G A Newhall was elected President. J S Hutchinson Secretary & Treasurer.

Minutes of meeting of April 28 1911 were approved as read.

It was voted to grant a five year lease of the Napa lot to John Head at $300. per annum. we to allow him out of first years rent $109. for repairs on house and stable.

The Board of Health having condemned as unsanitary the shacks on Van Ness Ave frontage of our lot, they demanding so many changes. as would cost as much as the buildings are worth. after a full discussion it was voted. that President Newhall be authorized to sell on best terms all the buildings on Van Ness Ave and Geary St. to have the lot cleared of debris and smoothed over & fenced in.

adjourned
                    J. S. Hutchinson
                         Secy
