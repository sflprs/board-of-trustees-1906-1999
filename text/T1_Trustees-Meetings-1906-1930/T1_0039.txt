May 1st [?] 1913.

At call of Prest Newhall trustees met at his office at 3. P.M to day

Present. Bishop Newhall, Hutchin- -son a quorum.

The thirty day option granted to Helbush at meeting of Mch 25th had been extended twice upon deposit each time of $50. Mr Helbush was now present with his attorney to discuss the changes he desired in the lease drawn by our attorney. Helbush presented a draft of lease he desired, to our attorney and this was brought in today but not acted upon.

In a letter to Prest Newhall Apl 24 [?] Mr Helbush stated that he proposed to incorpate a company to become our tenant. Our attorney presented. a lease somewhat re-modelled; essentially the same as that of Mch 25. naming the "Van Ness Realty Co" as lessees.

As Mr Helbush desired to have a strip 20 feet wide for a drive way in the rear of Van Ness buildings the Trustees voted to lease it at $60 per month.. and in the lease now agreed upon it is named at Parcel. 2

Meeting adjourned at 5. P.M to meet at call of Prest to sign lease
                    J. S. Hutchinson
                         Secy
