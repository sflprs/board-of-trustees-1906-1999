Clause No. 11. All buildings and improvements now standing and
situated upon said demised premises are the property of lessor and all
buildings and improvements that may be constructed or erected by lessee
upon the lands herein demised at any time during this lease shall imme-
diately vest in lessor and become a part of the realty, and lessee shall
retain no interest therein except the right of use and occupancy under
the leasehold interest created by this lease.

Subject to existing leases now outstanding upon the demised premises
and the rights of lessees thereunder, this lessee may and shall within
twenty-five (25) years from the date of this lease begin the construction
of and thereafter complete without unreasonable delay upon the whole or
some portion of the demised lands a building suitable to the neighbor-
hood in which it is erected at the time of its erection and which said
building shall be of a type of construction equivalent to or better than
the type which is now known as “Class A” as the same is now defined by
the ordinances of the City and County of San Francisco, State of Cali-
fornia. Said building when so constructed shall be free and clear of all
liens of contractors, subcontractors, mechanics, laborers, material men
and others and shall be of a reasonable value of at least Five Hundred
Thousand (500,000) Dollars.

Subject to said existing outstanding leases and the rights of the lessees

thereunder, this lessee shall at any time during the life of this lease have,

the right and privilege to remove or destroy any building, buildings or
improvements now or hereafter upon said demised premises, but only
upon the condition that this lessee will proceed without delay, imme-
diately following said removal or destruction, to erect upon said cleared
property a new building or buildings and to complete the same free of
liens and any and all cost, loss, damage, expense or liability of any kind
to lessor, as provided for in the bond hereinafter required; said new build-
ing or buildings shall have a construction value or values at least equal
to the fair value or values of the building, buildings or improvements
removed or destroyed, but before beginning the work in connection with
such removal or new construction lessee shall give to lessor sixty (60)
days’ notice of its intention and shall also furnish to lessor an accident
insurance policy indemnifying lessor against loss, cost, damage or acci-
dents occasioned by injury to persons, whether employees of any of the
said parties or members of the public, including any costs or counsel fees
incurred by lessor in defending itself against such claims, and also agrees
that prior to beginning any of such work of removal or new construc-
