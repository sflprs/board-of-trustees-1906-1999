Feby 26th 1913.

At call of Prest Newhall a meeting of the trustees was held at his office at 3. P.M today. Present trustees Bishop. Davis. Newhall. Hutchinson

Purpose of the meeting to consider a proposal by Mr Helbush who was present. for a lease of Van Ness lot.

He was told by Prest Newhall that we would not consider an offer of less than $1000 per month for 275 x 137. nett he to pay all taxes. for a 25 year lease.

Helbush offered $750 for 5 years $1000 for next 15 years $1250. for next 5 years totaling $300000 in 25 years. this was not accepted After a two hours discussion it was agreed to give Helbush a 30 day option to lease at $1000 per month. He paying $400. and $50 per week. if option extended 2 weeks. This time is to enable him to get plans of building & estimates
