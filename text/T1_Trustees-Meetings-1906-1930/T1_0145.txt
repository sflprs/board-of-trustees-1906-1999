It is the intent of the parties hereto that save only as to any tax as-

every character in relation to said demised property to the end that the
rental payable to lessor shall be net and free and clear of any and every
expense or deduction whatsoever. Lessee shall also pay any license tax
or other charge imposed by any lawful authority in respect to any area
or space adjacent to the demised premises and the right to the use of any
subsidewalk area shall be subject to the public power of license, regula-
tion or termination without affecting this lease or any rental thereunder.

Lessee shall have the right to contest or review or attempt to have
reduced or equalized by legal proceedings or in such manner as it may
deem suitable any tax or assessment levied, assessed or imposed upon said
real property or any portion thereof hereinbefore described or any im-
provements thereon, and lessor shall not pay the same if so notified in
writing during the time that lessee is so contesting, reviewing or attempt-
ing to have reviewed or equalized said tax or assessment.

The lessor, however, reserves the right to pay any tax or assessment,
whether contested by lessee or not, to prevent a final sale of said prop-
erty or a sale under conditions not permitting the redemption thereof,
and lessor may pay any such tax or assessment and thereafter have the
validity of such tax or assessment determined by a suit against the lessee
for the recovery of the amount so paid. In event of any sale of said
premises for non-payment of any tax or assessment payable by lessee
under the terms hereof, the lessee shall cause redemption to be made
before the time of expiration of the period of redemption and in no event
shall lessee permit or allow a sale of the demised premises or any portion
thereof or any improvements thereon for such tax or assessment in the
event that such sale is final or the right of redemption does not exist.
