[Book page 21]

Meeting May 10th continued

Kriess and Coombs lessees on Post street have removed their buildings

It was decided that we ask Contractor Parent to examine the [struck through: three [?]] (2) Post street houses and report to us as to their condition, with detail-ed estimate of cost to put them in order for renting.

The uncertainty as to improve-ments on Van Ness Ave is so great that it was concluded best to defer any action. A long lease of the Van Ness Ave frontage as is (ground lease) for 30 or 50 years was favored, but it is admitted that the present is not the time to offer it.

Baldwin & Howell were instructed to accept temporary tenants at nominal rents
                        J. S. Hutchinson
                             Secy
