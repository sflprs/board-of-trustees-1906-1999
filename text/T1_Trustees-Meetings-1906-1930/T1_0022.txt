Meeting of May 10th 1909

Trustees met to day at 11. A.M at the office of trustee Newhall Present trustees Newhall, Bishop, Davis. Kiel. Hutchinson full board

Minutes of last meeting Sept 21. 08 were read and approved.

As this was the first meeting since the annual meet-ing of the Society Jany 14. 1909 at which the members of the Board were all re-elected, for the purpose of organization trustee Bishop was called to preside. Proceeding to election of officers, Geo A Newhall was nomi-nated for President. J S Hutchinson for Secretary and Treasurer and upon vote were declared by the chairman duly elected.

On taking the chair President Newhall spoke of the near expiration of our leases on Block 83, A general discussion took place as to what could be done to produce income from the property. A representative of Messrs Baldwin & Howell was present by request and explained renting conditions.

That lessee Einstein's stores on Geary street have been vacant for months and he in default,
