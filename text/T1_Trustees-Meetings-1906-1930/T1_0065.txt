[Book page 63]

A special meeting of the Board of Trustees of the San Francisco Ladies Protection and Relief Society was held at the office of the President, Mr Geo. A. Newhall, on the fourteenth day of August 1916.

There were present trustees Newhall, Hutchinson and Lovell, absent trustee Keil.

Mr Newhall occupied the chair. and called the meeting to order.

The reading of the minutes of the previous meeting being postponed. Mr Newhall then announced the objects of the meeting. Which were, that since our last meeting, death had removed one of our trustees Horace Davis from our midst, and that the secretary be instructed to send a letter of sympathy to. Mr Norris K Davis his son, which being duly seconded, the Secretary was so instructed.

Mr Newhall then called the Boards attention to the reorganization suggested by the United R R of San Francisco. as the Society owned twenty of these bonds. After discussing the matter. Mr Hutchinson then offered the following resolution, which having been duly seconded, was unanimously carried.

Resolved, by the Board of Trustees of the San Francisco Ladies Protection and Relief Society that the Secretary be, and he is hereby, authorized and instructed to deposit the twenty bonds, of the United Rail Road of San Francisco, owned by this corporation, with the Union Trust Co of S. F. under their agreement with the reorganization Committee and to take the customary receipt for same.
