May 19th 1913.

The tenant in 1217 Post Street being advised that the house was to be moved some 40 feet up the street, indica-ted. her wish to continue as a tenant after the removal. Further, that if she could have a three years lease she would pay $90 per month for the two houses Nos 1215 & 1217 after they were moved, beginning with July. Baldwin & Howell had prepared a lease to this effect and presented it to Prest. Newhall. Mrs Emily Bellile [?] the lessee has been notified to vacate 1217. by the Van Ness Realty Co and will do so, returning to occupy both houses in their new position.

As Mrs Bellile [?] has been a satisfactory tenant for past year, it was deemed proper to make this lease to her. rather than await new tenants at an advanced rate. Lease signed by
                        G A Newhall Prest
                        J S Hutchinson Secy
