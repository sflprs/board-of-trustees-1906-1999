Augt 7th 1912

As per adjournment from July 31st trustees met at President Newhalls office. All the members present.

A lease prepared by client of Baldwin & Howell in terms as stated at our last meeting was read by Prest Newhall. and discussed, some minor changes proposed and it was agreed to submit it to attorney as to any legal questions involved

Adjourned to meet at call of Prest
                    J. S. Hutchinson
                         Secy

Sept 13th 1912

At call of Prest Newhall a meeting of the trustees was held at his office at 10.30 A.M.. Present Newhall Kiel and Hutchinson.
