May 23, 1906

A special meeting of the Board of Trustees was held this day at 2 O'Clk P.M. at the office of President Newhall. Present, trustees Bishop, Keil, Davis, Newhall, Hutchinson, a full board.

Secretary Hutchinson made a statement of the loss of all the records and papers of this Board in the great fire of April 18th last. That the large tin box containing the minute book and records, Horace Hawes deed, tax receipts, receipts for payments upon contracts, leases, etc. — historical sketches of the growth of the Society, — an accumula-tion of over fifty years. — was stored in an alleged fire proof safe in the Parrott Building 407 Montgomery street.

Some days after the fire, the safe was pulled from the ruins in the basement and broken open. Every thing it had contained that would burn was in ashes.

The financial loss is insignifi-cant, but the wiping out of the records of over half a century of planning for the helpless and needy is irrep-arable.
