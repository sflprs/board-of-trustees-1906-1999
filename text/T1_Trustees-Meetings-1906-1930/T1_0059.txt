It was with real regret that the trust-

who had faithfully acted in that capacity for
a quarter of a century, who had the arduous task
of gathering the minutes and re-establishing the
documents of the Society, which had been lost in
the great fire of April 18, 1906.

With President Newhall and the new Sec-
retary, and in the presence of ir. Hutchinson,
the documents, bonds and stock of the Society
were checked up in a safe deposit box, and all
found in absolutely perfect order. It is hoped
by the balance of the trustees that Mr. Hutchin-
son may continue as a valuable advisor of the
Board of Trustees.
