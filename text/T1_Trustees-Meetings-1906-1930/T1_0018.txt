Meeting of June 11th 1908

By order of President Newhall a meeting of Trustees was called for June 9th, failing of a quorum on that date, a call was issued for meeting this day June 11th at office of President Newhall.

Present Trustees. Newhall Keil and Hutchinson. a quorum

Minutes of last meeting on Feby 6. 1908 were read and ap-proved.

President Newhall stated that the lady managers had a cash balance of $29.000 to their credit with S. F. Savings Union and had requested the Trustees to invest $25.000 of it in bonds. He had learned that it was a term deposit requiring notice but he had promise of Cashier White to pay $10.000 on call.
