real estate agent. who he said was in waiting and desired to come before the Board. The letter was read, an offer of $7.500 pr annum for a 50 year lease of the Van Ness Ave frontage of our block 83. The bidder came in and stated that he proposed to erect two eight story apartment houses of brick occupying the frontage except a space of 35. feet in middle of the block this space left for light. In course of his talk he claimed that he could buy frontage on the avenue any where at $1000 pr foot. and as low as $600 but the lots not large enough for his purpose, After he withdrew the trustees had short discussion of the offer. If the frontage of 275 feet is worth $275.000 the offer is not equal to 3% pr annum. It was voted that Newhall and Keil be a committee to make enquiries among real estate agents as to prospects and terms of leases.

Trustee Keil had proposed to the managers the purchase of 15 acres at Ingleside, the ladies having refered it to this Board, President Newhall suggested that we take a look at the lot. and offered his auto. It was voted that we view it on Thursday Mch 17th. adjourned
                        J. S. Hutchinson Secy
