Meeting of May 23. 1906

The lease to Friedlander Goldstone Company was read and upon motion of trustee Bishop seconded by trustee Keil was ratified and then signed by all the trustees.

Trustee Keil proposed that Prest Newhall suggest to the Managers the desirability of setting apart a portion of the income from leases as a building fund. It was voted that such proposal be made.

Without further business the Board adjourned
                    J. S. Hutchinson
                         Secretary

[In margin: June 19.]

Meeting of June 19. 1906

The Lady Managers having issued an invitation to the Trustees to meet with them in conference at the "Home" on Franklin street on Tuesday June 19th at 10 O'Clk A.M. President Newhall called a special meeting of the Trustees at same hour and place. — Many ladies were present, and Trustees Newhall, Bishop, Keil and Hutchinson.

Statement was made to the meeting of the loss in the great fire of April 18th, of all records of the Society from the earliest in our history, — the Horace Hawes, and Keeley deeds insurance papers and le[ases ...] [continues on next page]
