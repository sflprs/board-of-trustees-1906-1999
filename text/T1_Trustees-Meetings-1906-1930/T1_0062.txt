Resolved that President Newhall and Secretary Lovell. be given the keys to the Safe Deposit box of the Society and that they have access thereto.

Director Keil then seconded this resolution which on vote was declared adopted.

The Secretary was then instructed to take up with I [?] Hayashi the question of leasing to him, the Society's property at Napa. and to report to the board later.

Mr J. S. Hutchinson, the attorney for the board was then instructed to collect what rent he could from John Head Jr. and cancel the lease with him on the Napa property.

Attorney J. S. Hutchinson then presented his charges for legal services to date amounting to three hundred & seventy five dollars and ten cents ($375 10/100). which was ordered paid.

There being no further business to come before the meeting, on motion, duly seconded it was declared adjourned subject to the call of the Chair.
                        Mansfield Lovell
                             Secretary
