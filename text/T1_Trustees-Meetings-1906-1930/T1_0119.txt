June 11, 1926.

A meeting of the Board of Trustees of the
San Francisco Ladies Protection and Relief Society
was held at the office of the President in the Newhall
Building, 260 California St., San Francisco, on Friday,
June 11, 1926, at 11:00 a.m.

The meeting was called to order by Mr. Norman
B. Livermore, Vice-President, in the absence of the
President, Mr. George A. Newhall, on account of sickness.

Present - Norman B. Livermore, Hugo D. Keil,
J. S. Hutchinson, Bruce Cornwall and
J. Cushing.

Absent - George A. Newhall, A. A. Smith.

The minutes of the meeting held on April 14,
1926, were read and approved as recorded.

In accordance with resolution passed at the meet-
ing of April 14, 1926, the Vice-President reported the

Upon motion duly made and seconded, the follow-
ing resolutions were unanimously adopted:

1. RESOLVED: That the donation from the Estate
of Teresa Bell amounting to $2083.33 be accepted by this
Society, and that a vote of thanks be accorded to the
Trustees of said Estate, The Secretary, Mr. Bruce
Cornwall was asked to convey this expression of thanks
to said Trustees.

2. RESOLVED: That the donation received from
the Estate of Teresa Bell be invested in high grade
bonds, and the Vice-President and Secretary are hereby
authorized and empowered to make such a purchase,
