Mr. Mansfield Lovell stated that he had had numerous conferences
with Mr. H. H. Helbush in reference to the Van Ness Avenue and Geary
Street property belonging to the Society, and that Mr. Helbush had
made a proposition to borrow from the Society the sum of Sixty thou-
sand (60,000) Dollars and give as security therefor all of his stock ra
in the Van Ness Realty Company, (a corporation), amounting to seven
hundred and forty-six (746) shares, -- the same consisting of
all of the shares of said corporation with the exception of four (4)
incorporators' shares, -- and the assignment of the present lease
of the Van Ness Avenue frontage. After a full discussion
of the matter, on motion, duly seconded, it was unanimously Resolved
that the Board of Trustees make a flat loan to said Helbush in the
sum of Sixty thousand (60,000) Dollars, for the term of five years,
at the rate of seven per cent. per annum, payable monthly, with the
privilege of repayment by said Helbush of one-half of said sum at any
time after two and one-half years from the date of the loan, -- said
loan to be secured by the pledge of all of said stock of the Van Ness
Realty Company, with the exception of the incorporators' shares, and
the assignment to this corporation of that certain lease dated May 6,
1913, between this Society and the Van WNesg Realty Company, and also
the lease hereinafter in these minutes referred to, between this Society
