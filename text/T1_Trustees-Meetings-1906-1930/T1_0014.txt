Meeting, Jany 15th, continued

By resolution of the Board at the meeting of Oct 26th last. the President and Treasurer were authorized to invest funds on deposit at our credit in Mercantile Trust Co.

They report purchase of $10,000 Bonds of United Railroads. through Daniel Meyer at 83.75

Adjourned
                        J. S. Hutchinson
                             Secy

Meeting of Sept. 19. 1907

Pursuant to notice mailed to each trustee a meeting was held this day at 11. A.M at the office of President Newhall Present. trustees Newhall, Bishop, Keil and Hutchinson.

Minutes of meeting of Jany 15 were read and approved.

Secretary stated pass book of this Society with Savings & Loan Society (Lux [?] trust) was destroyed in the fire of Apl 18, 1906. Upon his ap-plication recently for new book he was apprised that the depositors were desired to formally assent to a transfer of property from the Savings and Loan Society. to "The Savings and Loan Society" merely a change of name made
