On Monday May 21, after the fire which left our houses unharmed, had passed along the opposite side of Van Ness Ave. all danger to our property or the public had ceased. A company of dynamiters, it is alleged, under official instructions, placed a charge under the standing wall of the Concordia Club. The blast was of such force that three of our houses directly op-posite were wrecked, a fourth badly damaged, — window sash and doors of the six houses nearest Geary St were blown in, also: in school build-ing, and all windows of our main building, which faced east.

On Tuesday when contractor Parent came by appointment, the decision was to put the six houses nearest Geary street in order, the other four so badly damaged not worth repairing.
