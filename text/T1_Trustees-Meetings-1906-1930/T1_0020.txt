Meeting of September 21st 1908.

Pursuant to notice mailed to each trustee by order of Prest Newhall a meeting of the Board of Trustees was held this day at 10 o'clk A.M at Mercantile Safe Deposit Vault

Present. trustees Newhall Bishop. Keil and Hutchinson a quorum.

Minutes of last meeting June 11th 1908 were read and approved.

President Newhall stated that he had called the Board together to present a letter ad-dressed to him by the acting Secretary of the Lady Managers. This he read. It was enquiry if he could give the managers an idea as to amount of income they will receive when the leases on block 83 expire next year.

As business is moving down town from Van Ness Avenue rapidly, none of the trustees would venture to estimate. When stores in the neighborhood are vacated they remain so.
