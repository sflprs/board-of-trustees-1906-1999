Meeting of Feby 6. 1908.

Pursuant to notice mailed to each trustee, a meeting was held today at 11. A.M at the office of Presdt. Newhall

Present: Trustees Newhall. Bishop and Hutchinson; a quorum.

Minutes of our last meeting Sept 19. 1907. were read and ap-proved.

Trustees Bishop and Newhall a committee reported purchase of thirty shares (30) of stock of the Bank of California at 335,

A letter from Daniel Einstein lessee of a frontage on our lot on Geary street, asking a reduction in the terms of the lease, was read. In the discussion that ensued it was shown that negotiation of the lease was made by Baldwin & Howell who were entirely famil-iar with going rates at the time. Our lease was satisfactory to lessor and we had no inten-tion of taking a share in his risks. Under all the circumstances it was dec[ided] [end of word hidden at the page edge] not to reduce the rent to Einstein

This item concludes the business of the Board for 1907.
