March 14th 1910.

Pursuant to notice mailed to each trustee a meeting of the Board was held this day at the office of President Newhall at 3.15. P.M.

Present trustees Newhall. Bishop Keil and Hutchinson a quorum

Being the first meeting since the annual meeting of the society when all the members of the Board of 1909 were re-elected, for purpose of organization trustee Bishop was called to the chair. Trustee Keil nominated for president G. A. Newhall. for secretary and treasurer J. S. Hutchinson upon a vote being taken the chair declared them duly elected to the respective offices for the ensuing year.

President Newhall on taking the chair, called for the reading of the minutes of last meetings.

Secretary read minutes of meeting of May 10. 1909. and of special meeting Sept 7. 1909. both of which were approved.

Treasurer read his report for past year showing balance cash on hand in Mercantile Trust Co at this date $1835.99.. ordered filed.
