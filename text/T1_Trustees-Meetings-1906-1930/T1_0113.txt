The Secretary stated that it had been necessary to
call this special meeting of the Trustees, as there were
several matters of importance, upon which action had to be

taken without delay.

Upon motion duly made and seconded the following
resolutions were unanimously adopted:

1, RESOLVED: That the right of access to and control of
the contents of Safe No. 4015, Mercantile Trust Company,
Safe Deposit Department, and the right to remove the whole
or any part of said contents shall be and is hereby given to
the following persons separately or togethers:

George A. Newhall, Norman B. Livermore, Bruce Cornwall.

and such rights shall continue until said Safe Deposit
Department shall be in receipt of a written revocation of

such authorization.

2. RESOLVED: That the Vice President, Mr. Norman B,
Livermore, and Secretary, Mr. Bruce Cornwall, be and the sane
are hereby authorized and empowered as and for the act and
deed of this Society to bring suit against Mr. H.H. Helbush
to protect the interests of the Society in the note due it
from said H.H, Helbush, or to take such other action, includ-
ing the taking of a new note from said H.H. Nelbush if with
the advice of the Attorney for the Society, such action is
deemed advisable,
