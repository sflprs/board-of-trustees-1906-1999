April 14, 1926,

A meeting of the Board of Trustees of the San
Francisco Ladies Protection and Relief Society was held
at the office of the President in the Newhall Building,
260 California St., San Francisco, on Wednesday, April 14th,

1926 at 2:00 p.m.

The meeting was called to order by Mr. Norman B,.
Livermore, Vice-President, in the absence of the President,
Mr. George A. Newhall, on account of sickness.

Present - Norman B. Livermore, Hugo D. Keil,
J. S. Hutchinson, and Bruce Cornwall.

Absent - George A. Newhall, A. A. Smith, and
J. Cushing.

The minutes of the meeting held on March 10,
1926, were read and approved as recorded,

The Secretary read a letter received from Miss
Lottie Yoods, chairman of the Finance Committee of the
Board of Lady Managers, advising the Trustees of their
willingness to endeavor to operate the "New Home" on the
budget sum suggested by the Trustees, namely $1462.31
per month. On motion duly made and seconded, the letter
from Miss Lottie Woods was ordered placed in the minutes.

Upon motion duly made and seconded, the following
resolutions were also unanimously adopted:

1. RESOLVED: That the Vice-President and
Secretary be and they are hereby authorized to accept
payment from H. H. Helbush of the balance due under his
note with interest at seven percent per annum which he
has tendered to the Society, and to refund to him the
amount which according to the Society's figures he has
overpaid, And the Vice-President and Secretary are
further authorized and empowered to return to said ti.H.
Helbush said note together with certain stock certifi-
cates of the Van Ness Realty Co. held as collateral se-
curity, and to complete and deliver to said H. H. Helbush
a re-assignment of that certain lease between H. H.
Helbush and this Soqciety also held as security.

2. RESOLVED: That the Vice-President and the
Secretary be and they are hereby authorized and empowered
to invest in bonds the available funds now lying to the
Society's credit in its Commercial Account in the Mer-
cantile Trust Company.

No further business appearing, the meeting on
motion adjourned.
