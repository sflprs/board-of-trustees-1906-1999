May 7, 1952

A special meeting of the Board of Directors of
the San Francisco Ladies' Protection and Relief Society was
called by the President and held at the American Trust
Company, 464 California Street, San Francisco, on Wednesday,
May 7, 1952, at 3:00 P.M.

The President, Mr. Moore, called the meeting to

order.

There were present the full Board.

The President referred to the death of Mr. E. VY.
Krick on May 4, 1952 and noted that he had been a member of
this Board since December 7, 1943.

The following resolution was then presented to the

meeting:

WHEREAS, on the fourth day of May, Nineteen Hundred
and Fifty-Two there passed from this life

EDWIN V. KRICK
WHEREAS, since the year nineteen hundred and forty-
three Mr, Krick had served as a member of the Board
of Directors of the San Franciswm Ladies' Protection
and Relief Society and as its Treasurer during most
of that time and

WHEREAS, during that period he gave generously of

his time, wisdom and abilities to promote the material
interests of this institution and to assist in solving
the many problems presented to this Board and

WHEREAS, we his fellow directors, in our association
with him have been deeply impressed by his high

character, integrity, fairness and unvarying cheerful
demeanor

NOW, THEREFORE, in recognition and appreciation of
these facts and of the irreparable loss suffered by
this Society in the passing of Mr. Krick, be and it is
hereby *

RESOLVED, that we now express and record our deep
sense of appreciation of the fine and unselfish service
rendered to this Society by Mr. Krick and we further
express aml record our profound sorrow and regret at
his passing from us and be it further

RESOLVED, that this resolution be spread_in full upon
the minutes of the Directors of the San Francisco
Ladies' Protection and Relief Society and that a copy
hereof be forwarded in expression of our deep sympathy
to the family of Mr. Krick.

No further business appearing, the meeting upon notic:

Secretary
