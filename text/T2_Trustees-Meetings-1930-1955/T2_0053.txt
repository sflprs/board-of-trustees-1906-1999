March 2nd, 1943.

The regular quarterly meeting of the Board of Dir-
ectors of the San Francisco Ladies! Protection and Relief
Society was held at the office of Mr. N. B. Livermore, Presi-
dent, 216 Pine Street, San Francisco, on Tuesday, March 2nd,
1943, at 2:15 pom.

The President, Mr. N. B. Livermore, called the meet-
ing to order.

Present: WN. B. Livermore, J. S. Hutchinson, M. R.- Gibbons
and W. P. Johnson.

Absent: J. Cushing and Bruce Cornwall.

The minutes of the Directors meeting held on December
lst, 1942 were read and approved as recorded.

The President laid before the meeting the last report
from Mr. Harris dated February lst, 1943, on the Society's
portfolio, and considerable discussion was held on the present
condition of the investments. It was agreed to request Mr.
Harris to make his quarterly reports coincide with the quarterly
meeting of this Board.

Upon motion of Mr. Hutchinson, seconded by Dr. Gibbons,
it was

RESOLVED: That the action of the Finance Committee

in making the following sales and purchases is hereby ratified,
approved and confirmed.

The question was then brought up as to the amount of
fire insurance which should be carried on the Marina Building
and Contents, as the Board of Fire Underwriters were requesting
their periodic revaluation. The Fireman's Fund Insurance Company
adjuster has recommended that in view of increased costs the
amount of coverage be increased from {153,000.00 to 150,000.00.

It was unanimously agreed that the fire insurance be
increased to $150,000.00 as recommended.
