OF PRELIMINARY RESOLUTION
OF BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF THE SAN FRANCISCO
LADIES’ PROTECTION AND RELIEF SOCIETY
AUTHORIZING NEGOTIATION OF LEASE

ResoLvep: That this corporation, DIRECTORS OF THE SAN FRAN-
CISCO LADIES’ PROTECTION AND RELIEF SOCIETY, purchase from
LERNER SHOPS OF NEVADA, INC., the building and land known as 151 to
155 North Virginia Street, in the City of Reno, State of Nevada, for the sum of
$300,000.00, provided that said Lerner Shops of Nevada, Inc., shall by a Lease
satisfactory to this corporation, and guaranteed on behalf of the Tenant by
LERNER STORES CORPORATION, lease said property from this corpora-
tion for a term of fifty years, such Lease to provide, among other things, that
the net annual rental to be paid to this corporation shall be the equivalent of
5Y4% of the purchase price plus 514% of so much of $100,000.00 as may be
advanced by this corporation to make alterations in or to said building. Said
rental to be payable in equal monthly installments and to be net to this corpora-
tion, so that in addition to said rental the Tenant shall pay promptly when due
all taxes, assessments and expenses pertaining to said property, including all
expenses in connection with maintenance and repair, and including also the
cost of all fire insurance and public liability insurance and all replacements and
alterations on said property shall be paid by said Tenant.

RESOLVED FURTHER: That the President and Secretary are hereby authorized
and directed, in the name and on behalf of this corporation, to forthwith nego-
tiate and determine the terms, provisions and conditions of the said Lease, and
if and when the terms and provisions of said Lease are satisfactory to this cor-
poration, then contemporaneously with the execution of a good and sufficient
deed granting and conveying to this corporation a good and merchantable title
to said property, free and clear of all incumbrances, other than party wall agree-
ments and an entrance, stairway and corridor agreement now of record, and
subsisting Leases, they are authorized, for and on behalf of this corporation,
to execute the Lease aforesaid upon the due execution thereof by the Tenant.
