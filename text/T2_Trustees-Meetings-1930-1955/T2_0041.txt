December 14th, 1939.

A special meeting of the Board of Directors of the
San Francisco Ladies! Protection and Relief Society was held at
the office of Mr. Bruce Cornwall, Vice-President, 57 Sutter Street,
San Francisco, on Thursday, December 14th, 1939, at 12:15 p.m.

The President, Mr. N. B. Livermore, called the meeting
to order.

Present: N. B. Livermore, Bruce Cornwall, J. S. Hutchinson,
W. P. Johnson, J. Cushing and M. R. Gibbons

The minutes of the directors! meetings held on May 5th,
1939 and August Srd, 1939 were read and approved as recorded.

The President again reviewed the activities of the
Finance Committee since the last time any action had been taken
by this Board, and upon motion of Judge Johnson, seconded by
Dr. Gibbons, it was
