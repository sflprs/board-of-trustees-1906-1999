December 5, 1949

The regular quarterly meeting of the Board of
Directors of the San Francisco Ladies' Protection and
Relief Society was held at the office of the American Trust
Company, 464 California Street, San Francisco, California,
on Monday, December 5, 1949 at 2:15 P.M.

The President, Mr. Cornwall, called the meeting
to order,
Present:

Bruce Cornwall Ferard Leicester

E. V. Krick David L. White

Joseph R. Moore, Jr.

Also, Mr. Mansfield, Investment Counsel
Absent:

J. S. Hutchinson

John Cushing

Norman B, Livermore

The minutes of the regular quarterly meeting

held on October 3, 1949 were read and approved as recorded,

WHEREAS, on the eighth day of November, nineteen hundre:

and forty-nine, there passed from this life:
DR. MORTON R. GIBBONS

WHEREAS, since the year nineteen hundred and
thirty-eight, Dr. Gibbons had served as a member of the
Board of Directors of the San Francisco Ladies' Protection
and Relief Society and

WHEREAS, during that period he gave generously
of his time, wisdom and abilities in assisting to solve
the many problems presented to this Board in promoting
the welfare of this Society

NOW, THEREFORE, in recognition and appreciation
of these facts and of the loss suffered by the Society
be it and it is hereby

RESOLVED: That this Board does herewith record
their recognition of the faithful service rendered by
