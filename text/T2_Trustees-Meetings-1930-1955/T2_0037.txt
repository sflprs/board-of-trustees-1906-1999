The President then read a letter from Mrs. Deering of

the subject of circulars to be sent out by the Board of Lady
Managers to attract donations to the Society through attorneys
and others dealing with wills and estates. The President asked
that Judge Johnson, assisted by the Secretary, Mr. Hutchinson,
take this matter in hand, confer with the Lady Managers and give
them the assistance desired.

The Secretary reported on the condition of affairs at
Camp Smith and was given authority if he saw fit to have any of
the dilapidated buildings removed.

No further business appearing, the meeting upon motion

Da

Secretary.

adjourned.
