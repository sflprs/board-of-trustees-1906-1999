December lst, 1942.

The regular quarterly meeting of the Board of Directors
of the San Francisco Ladies! Protection and Relief Society was
held at the office of Mr. N. B. Livermore, President, 216 Pine
Street, San Francisco, on Tuesday, December lst, 1942, at 2:15
pem.

The President, Mr. N. B. Livermore, called the meeting
to order.

Present: N. B. Livermore, Bruce Cornwall, J. S. Hutchinson,
and M. R. Gibbons.

Absent: W. P. Johnson and J. Cushing.

The minutes of the Directors meeting held on May 19th,
1942 were read and approved as recorded.

The President reported on the state of the Society's
portfolio of securities and commented on the fact that after
regular discussions with the Investment Counsellor, the Finance
Committee had made many changes, particularly sales, but also
that a considerable investment had been made in Government Bonds.

Upon motion of Dr. Gibbons, seconded by Mr. Hutchinson,
it was

RESOLVED: That the action of the Finance Committee

in making the following sales and purchases is hereby ratified,
approved and confirmed.
