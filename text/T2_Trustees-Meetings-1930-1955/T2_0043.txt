January 16th, 1941.

A special meeting of the Board of Directors of the
San Francisco Ladies' Protection and Relief Society was held at
the office of Mr. N. B. Livermore, President, 216 Pine Street,
San Francisco, on Thursday, January 16th, 1941, at 12:15 p.m.
The President, Mr. N. B. Livermore, called the meeting
to order.

Present: N. B. Livermore, J. S. Hutchinson, W. P. Johnson
and M. R. Gibbons.

Absent: Bruce Cornwall and J. Cushing.

The minutes of the directors' meeting held on December
14th, 1939 were read and approved as recorded.

The President reported that the Committee appointed to
engage an expert consultant to assist the Finance Committee in
handling the Society's portfolio, had made an exhaustive study and
investigation, and had finally engaged Mr. Everett L. Harris as
consultant, and that his fee would be based on one quarter of one
percent of the amount of the securities in the fund to be admin-
istered. The fee would be payable quarterly in advance, and at
the present time this quarterly fee amounts to $125.00.

The President further reported that he had had consider-
able discussion already with Mr. Harris, and as a result some
changes were recommended in the portfolio.

Upon motion of Mr. J. S. Hutchinson, seconded by Judge
Johnson, it was

RESOLVED: That the action of the Finance Committee in

making the following sales and purchases is hereby ratified,
approved and confirmed.
