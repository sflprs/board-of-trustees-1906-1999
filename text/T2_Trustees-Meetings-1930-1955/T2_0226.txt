San Francisco Ladies' Protection and Relief Society was held
at the office of the American Trust Company, 464 California
Street, San Francisco, on Wednesday, June 8, 1955 at 3:00
o'clock p.m.

The President suggested that the architect be authorized
to proceed with certain surveys setting out the proposed new
building and upon motion duly made this authority was given, the
cost of such work meantime not to exceed $500.00.

Upon motion duly made seconded and carried the president
was authorized to sell from $100,000.00 to $150,000.00 of securities
now in the portfolio, the funds to be used for the new building,

Such funds until required are to be deposited in savings
accounts at various banks in the name of Interim Protection and
Relief Society, the surviving corporation after the merger is

completed,
