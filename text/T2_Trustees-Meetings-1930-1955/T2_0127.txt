1907, between Washoe County Bank and A. E. Cheney, to which reference is
hereby made, and said Tenant has accepted said Lease and the premises therein
described subject to said Party Wall Agreements.

thereto are allowed to make certain alterations and extensions in said Party
Walls, and the expense of such alterations and extensions is to be equally borne
by the respective owners of said property.
