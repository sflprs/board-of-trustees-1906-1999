RESOLVED that this corporation continue a Savings
Account (General Deposit) with THE SAN FRANCISCO BAM and
that withdrawals of any funds which this corporation may
at any time have on deposit therein may be made by, and
withdrawal orders or receipts thereof may be signed by any
two (2) of the following:

Barrett G, Hindes President
John E, Cushing Vice President
J. S. Hutchinson Secretary
Bradley B, Brown Treasurer

RESOLVED, further, that said Bank is authorized to
honor and pay all withdrawal and other orders signed as pro-

vided herein, whether or not payable to any person signing
them.

RESOLVED, further, that checks, drafts and other instru-
ments payable to this corporation may be indorsed for
deposit in said account by any of the persons named above or
by any other employee oragent of this corporation, either in
writing or by stamp and either with or without the designa-
tion of the person so indorsing.

RESOLVED, further, that the authority conferred hereby
shall remain in force until written notice of the revoca-
tion thereof by the Board of Directors of this corporation

shall have been received by said Bank at the office at which
said account is kept.

RESOLVED, further, that the certification of the secreter
or an assistant secretary of this corporation as to the

persons authorized to sign and their signatures shall be Dini.
ing upon this corporation.

RESOLVED, that AMERICAN TRUST COMPANY be, and it is
hereby selected as a Depositary of the funds of this Corpor-
ation, and that checks or drafts withdrawing said funds may
be signed by any two of the following:

Barrett G, Hindes President
John E, Cushing Vice President
J. S. Hutchinson Secretary
Bradley B, Brown Treasurer

FURTHER RESOLVED; that AMERICAN TRUST COMPANY is
authorized to honor and pay any and all checks and drafts
of this Corporation signed as provided herein, whether or
not payable to the person or persons signing them; and that
checks, drafts, bills of exchange, and other evidences of
indebtedness may be endorsed for deposit to the account of
this corporation by any of the foregoing or by any other
employee or agent of this corporation, and may be endorsed

in writing or by stamp and with or without the designation
of the person so endorsing.

FURTHER RESOLVED; that the authority hereby conferred
shall remain in force until written notice of the revocation
by the Board of Directors of this Corporation shall have
been received by said depositary at the office at which the
account is kept; and that the certification of the secretar’
or an assistant secretary as to the continuing authority of
this resolution and the persons authorized to sign and their
Signatures, shall be binding upon this corporation.
