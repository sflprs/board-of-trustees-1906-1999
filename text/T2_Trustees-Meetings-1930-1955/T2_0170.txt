The President stated that the Society owns 162
shares of Pacific Gas and Hlectric Company, common stock, and
are now being given rights to permit purchase of twenty-three
(23) additional shares. Upon motion duly made and seconded
authority was given to purchase enough additional rights at
the market to enable the Society to purchase enough shares to
make their holding two hundred (200) shares,

A discussion was held regarding the increased values
of the Marina and Van Ness Buildings for fire insurance pur-
poses, as outlined by our insurance agents.

The President was authorized to increase the coverage
as recommended with the suggestion that the co-insurance rate
be checked for the best percentage of coverage for our
protection.

The President referred to the request of the Board
of managers for an increase in their monthly operating fund
from $3,500. to $5000. to cover greatly increased cost of
operation, He also stated that the Board had requested $8000,
to increase their working fund, $4000. of which would be used
for capital replacements and that the Executive Committee
had authorized the transfer of this Fund to the Board of
manager's account.

After considerable discussion upon motion duly made
and seconded the action of the Executive Committee in trans-
ferring $8,000. was ratified and approved and the monthly
allotment for operation of the tome was raised from $3,500.
to $5,000. retroactive to January 1, 1951, with the under-
standing that the situation be reviewed after six months.
