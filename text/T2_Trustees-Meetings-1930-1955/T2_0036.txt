December 8th. 1938

A special meeting of the Board of Directors! of the |
San Francisco Ladies! Protection and Relief Society was hela at |
the office of Mr. Bruce Cornwall, Vice-President, 57 Sutter Str
San Francisco, on Thursday, December 8th. 1938 at 12.15 p.m,

The President, Mr. N. B. Livermore called the meeting

to order.

Present: N. B. Livermore, Bruce Cornwall, J. S. Hutchinson,
W..P. Johnson and J. Cushing.

Absent: M. R. GibbonsSr.

The minutes of the directors' meeting held on August
9th. 1938 were read and approved as recorded.

The President outlined what action had been taken by
the Finance Committee since the last meeting and asked approval
of the transactions concluded. Upon motion of Mr. Cushing,
seconded by Mr. Hutchinson it was

RESOLVED: That the action of the Finance Committee in
making the following purchases and sales, is hereby ratified,
approved and confirmed.

Purchase.

Phillips Petroleum Co. 3% Conv. Debentures

5 bonds @ 1074 $ 5,437.

Sale.

Shell Union 011 Corpn. 33% S. F. Debentures |

5 bonds @ 104 1/8 $ 5,278.

The Vice-President, Mr. Cornwall, expressed the opin:
that the Society should retain the services of an expert consul
to advise the Finance Committee in the matter of the portfolio
securities. After considerable discussion as to the cost aaa
merits of such a procedure, the President asked all present to
give this matter careful consideration, with a view to reaching
a decision at the next meeting of the Board.

The President announced that the Society had receive:
a check from the Estate of Anna Beaver amounting to $4,596.50,
which was to be used by the Society at the Trustees discretion,
After some discussion as to the investment of this sum, ON mott:
of Mr. Cornwall, seconded by Mr. Cushing, it was

Resolved; That the President write to the Executon,

of said Estate accepting this donation, and that the Finance
Committee purchase first rate bonds with the funds.
