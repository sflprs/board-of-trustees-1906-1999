June 6th, 1944.

The regular quarterly meeting of the Board of
Directors of the San Francisco Ladies' Protection and Relief
Society was called for Tuesday, June 6th, 1944, at 2:15 p.m.,
at the office of Mir. N. 3. Livermore, President, 216 Pine
Street, San Francisco, in accordance with resolution passed on
May 19th, 1942, but as a quorum was not obtainable, no meeting
was held.

September 5th, 1944.

Society was called for Tuesday, September 5th, 1944, at 2:15
p.m., at the office of Mr. N. B. Livermore, President, 216
