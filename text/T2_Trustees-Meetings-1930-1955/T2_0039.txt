The President then stated that he had a communication
from Mrs. Deering, in which she enclosed two bills covering
replacements at the Home for new boiler equipment $492. and
additional elevator facilities $685.97, Total $077.97.

After considerable discussion as to the advisability of the
Board paying such items if the Board of Lady Managers have any
funds available out of their budget allowance, it was agreed
that they should be paid.

The question of the retention by the Board of Lady
Managers of any surplus funds out of their budget allowance was
then thoroughly discussed, and upon motion of Dr. Gibbons,
seconded by Mr. Cushing it was

RESOLVED: That the President communicate with Mrs.
Deering and represent to her that it is the feeling of tnis
Board that it is inadvisable for the Board of Lady Managers to
accumulate funds out of their budget allowance to a greater
amount than #2500 in their bank account and that when 2 greater
amount is accumulated it is believed that the surplus should be

returned to this Board for investment.
