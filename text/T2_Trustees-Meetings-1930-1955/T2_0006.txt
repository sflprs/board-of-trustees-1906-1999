Ky ¥ DECEMBER, NINETEEN HUNDRED AND TWENTY-NINE
THERE PASSED FROM THIS LIFE

GEORGE A.NEWHALL

WHEREAS, MR. NEWHALL HAD THEN SERVED AS A MEM-
BER OF THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF THE SAN FRANCISCO LADIES’
PROTECTION AND RELIEF SOCIETY FOR MORE THAN THIRTY YEARS
AND DURING MOST OF THAT TIME AS PRESIDENT OF SUCH BOARD;
AND

WHEREAS, DURING ALL OF THAT PERIOD HE GENEROUSLY
GAVE OF HIS TIME, WISDOM AND ABILITIES TO PROMOTE THE
MATERIAL INTERESTS AND GENERAL WELFARE OF THIS INSTITU-

TION, THUS ENABLING ITS BOARD OF MANAGERS TO MAINTAIN

AND EXTEND ITS BENEFICENT PURPOSES; AND

WHEREAS, WE, HIS FELLOW DIRECTORS, IN OUR ASSOCIA-
TION WITH HIM HAVE BEEN EVER DEEPLY IMPRESSED BY HIS HIGH
CHARACTER, STERLING INTEGRITY, CLEAR SENSE OF FAIRNESS AND
UNVARYING COURTEOUS AND CHEERFUL DEMEANOR;

NOW, THEREFORE, IN RECOGNITION AND APPRECIA-
TION OF THESE FACTS AND OF THE IRREPARABLE LOSS SUFFERED
BY THIS SOCIETY IN THE PASSING OF MR. NEWHALL, BE IT AND IT
IS HEREBY

RESOLVED, THAT IN RESPECTFUL MEMORY OF OUR LATE
PRESIDENT, GEORGE A. NEWHALL, WE NOW EXPRESS AND RECORD
OUR DEEP SENSE OF APPRECIATION OF HIS FINE, UNSELFISH SERV-
ICE TO THIS SOCIETY, AND WE FURTHER EXPRESS AND RECORD
FOR THE SOCIETY AND OURSELVES PROFOUND SORROW AND RE-
GRET AT HIS PASSING FROM US; AND BE IT FURTHER

RESOLVED, THAT THIS RESOLUTION BE SPREAD IN FULL
UPON THE MINUTES OF THE DIRECTORS OF THE SAN FRANCISCO
LADIES’ PROTECTION AND RELIEF SOCIETY AND THAT A COPY
HEREOF BE FORWARDED IN EXPRESSION OF OUR DEEP SYMPATHY
TO THE FAMILY OF MR. NEWHALL.

DIRECTORS OF SAN FRANCISCO LADIES’
PROTECTION AND RELIEF SOCIETY
