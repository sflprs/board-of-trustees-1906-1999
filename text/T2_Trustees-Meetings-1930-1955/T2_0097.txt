The Attorney stated that in the matter of the increase
in the number of Directors or Trustees it would be desirable to
pass a resolution amending the Articles of Incorporation, where-
upon on motion duly made and seconded the following resolution

was carried:

WHEREAS, the members of this Society have consented to

an Amendment to the Articles of Incorporation of this
Society increasing the number of Directors or Trustees

of this Society from seven to nine, and have requested
this Board of pass a Resolution so amending said Articles,
and have given their written consent to the Amendment
hereinafter made or provided for:

NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED that the articles of
Incorporation of this corporation be amended by adding
an Article thereto to read as follows, to wit:

"The number of Directors or Trustees of this
Corporation shall be nine."

BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED that the President or a Vice-
President and the Secretary or an Assistant Secretary
of this Society be, and they are hereby, authorized
and directed to sign and verify by their respective
oaths and to file a Certificate in the form and
manner required by Section 3672 of the Corporations
Code of the State of California, and in general to do
any and all things necessary to effect said amendment
in accordance with said Section 3672.
The question of present investment counsel and whether
a change should be made to a San Francisco firm was discussed.
The President then appointed a committee composed of lr. hrick,
ir. Cushing and Dr. Gibbons to look into the matter and report
at the next meeting of this Board in June.
The President also referred to the vacancy on this
Board and the Assistant Secretary was instructed to write each
member to give consideration to this matter and be prepared to
discuss same at the next meeting.
