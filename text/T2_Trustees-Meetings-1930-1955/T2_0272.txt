In accordance with notice duly given, a special

neeting of the members of San Francisco Ladies' Protection
and Relief Society was held at 3400 Laguna Street, San
Francisco on Tuesday, December 6, 1955 at 10:00 o'clock
A.M.

Mrs. Warren C. Perry occupied the chair and
called the meeting to order.

After her opening remarks, stating the reason for
the meeting, she requested Nr. Hindes to conduct the rest
of the meeting.

Mr. Hindes outlined the proposed merger and the
responsibilities to be undertaken to make it a success and
stated the financial position was strong. He discussed in
general the numbers and types of people whom they hoped to
care for when plans were completed.

He then stated that it would be in order to have
motion to approve of agreement of merger, whereupon on
motion of Mrs. Wm, K. Bowes, seconded by Mrs. Donald Craig,
the following resolution was unanimously carried:

Resolution and
‘ Agreement of Merger Attached)

Mrs, Perry read to the meeting a letter received
from Mr. Stetson Hindes in which he stated he wished to donate
$15,000.00 in memory of Mrs, Hindes to rebuild and furnish
the stone cottage on the grounds for use as the superintendent's
dwelling,

Upon motion duly made, the meeting was recessed for
fifteen minutes to permit the members to hold a similar meet-
ing of the members of Interim Protection and Relief society
to vote on the agreement of merger.

Cn resumption, the meeting took the form of the
regular meeting of the Board of sianagers, which is recorded

in the minutes kept by the secretary of that Board.
