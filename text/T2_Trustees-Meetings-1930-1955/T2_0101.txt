The President reported that in accordance with
recommendation made at the last meeting he had communicated
with Harris & Co, regarding the reopening of their San Francisco
office. He read a letter received from Mr, Harris in reply
which gave no indication when such an office might be reopened.

After discussion it was agreed that the recommendation
of the Committee be adopted and that Stephenson & llansfield be
appointed as Investment Counsel to this Board. The President
was requested to advise Mr. Harris of this decision and notify
Stephenson & Mansfield of their appointment, arranging a
suitable time for the change and confirming the fee to be charged.
