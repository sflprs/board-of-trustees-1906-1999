March 5, 1951

The regular quarterly meeting of the Board of
Directors of the San Francisco Ladies' Protection and Relief
Society, was held at the office of American Trust Company,
46, California Street, San Francisco, California, on Monday,
March 5, 1951 at 2:15 p.m.
The President, Mr. Moore, called the meeting to order,
PRESENT :
