CERTIFIED COPY OF RESOLUTION OF
LERNER SHOPS OF NEVADA, INC.

I, T. C. O'BRIEN, Assistant Secretary of LERNER SHOPS OF NEVADA,
INC,, do hereby certify that at a special meeting of the Board of Directors of
said corporation, duly held on the 15th day of November, 1948, at which
meeting a quorum was present, a resolution, of which the following is a true
copy, was unanimously adopted:

“RESOLVED: That this corporation sell to Directors of Ladies’
Protection and Relief Society, a Californian Corporation, the
building and land owned by this corporation and known as 151
to 155 North Virginia Street in the City of Reno, State of Nevada,
for the sum of Three Hundred Thousand Dollars ($300,000)
and that, concurrently with said sale, this corporation shall lease
said property from the aforesaid Society for a term of 50 years
at a net rental of Sixteen Thousand Five Hundred Dollars
($16,500) per annum. The said Lease shall provide that the
Tenant, commencing in July, 1951, shall make substantial struc-
tural alterations to the building so as to convert same into a
modern commercial building and shall complete such construc-
tion by June 30, 1952. The Landlord shall reimburse the Tenant
for the cost of such work up to the sum of One Hundred Thou-
sand Dollars ($100,000) and thereafter the rental shall be
annually increased by 514% of the amount of such contribution
by the Landlord. The Tenant shall pay all taxes, insurance pre-
miums and shall make all required repairs. FURTHER RESOLVED:
