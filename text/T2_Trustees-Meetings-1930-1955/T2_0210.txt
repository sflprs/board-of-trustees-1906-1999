February 23, 1955

A special meeting of the Board of Directors of San

Francisco Ladies' Protection and Relief Society was held at

the office of the American Trust Company, 464 California Street,

San Francisco, on Wednesday, February 23, 1955 at 2:15 P.M,

order,

PRESENT:

ABSENT:

The President, Mr. Hindes, called the meeting to

Barrett G. Hindes
Joseph A. Moore, Jr.
Bradley B. Brown
Ferard Leicester
John Cushing

David L. White

J. S. Hutchinson
Bruce Cornwall
Chalmers G. Graham

Mr, White reported that Coldwell, Banker & Co, had

negotiated a lease with Claude J. Howard for a portion of the

Van Ness Block, known as 1156 Geary Street, for one year from

January 1, 1955, at $125.00 per month.

Upon motion duly made and seconded, the action of the

officers of this Board in signing this lease, a copy of which

is made a part of these minutes, is hereby ratified, approved

and confirmed,

By unanimous agreement it was decided to transfer

$10,000.00 from the Commercial Bank account to a Savings Account,

No further business appearing, the meeting adjourned,
