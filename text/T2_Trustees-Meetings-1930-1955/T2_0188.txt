The President reported that the Society had
received a stock dividend from National City Bank of eight
shares plus a fraction. As the Society now owns 100
shares of this stock it was unanimously agreed to purchase

sufficient to bring the holding to an even 110 shares,

New plans covering the building to be erected
by Lerner Shops on the Reno property were presented to the
meeting for approval, These plans are to replace plans

presented previously by Lerner's for alterations. No speci-

fications have been presented. Upon motion duly made and
seconded the plans as shown were approved,

The President advised that owing to changes in
the officers, new signature cards would have to be filed
covering the various bank accounts and safe deposit box, On
motion duly made and seconded it was:
