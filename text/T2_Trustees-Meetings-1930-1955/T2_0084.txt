The Regular Quarterly meeting of the Board of
Directors of the San Francisco Ladies Protection and Relief
Society was held at the office of the President, Mr, N, B.
Livermore, 216 Pine Street, San Francisco, on Tuesday,
March 4, 1947 at 2:15 P.M.

The President, Mr. Livermore, called the meeting

to order.

PRESENT:
N. B. Livermore,, W. P. Johnson E.V.Krick
J. S. Hutchinson M. R. Gibbons

ABSENT :
Bruce Cornwall J. Cushing

The minutes of the regular quarterly meeting held
on December 3, 1946 were read and approved as recorded,

The President stated that another letter had been
TFeceived from San Jose Water Works making an offer for the
Camp Smith property of $125.00 per acre. As this offer was
so low and as the cost of carrying the property was negligible
he suggested that no action be taken on the offer. This
suggestion was agreed to.

A letter was read from Coldwell, Banker & Co,
regarding repairs to the parapet wall on the Van Ness Avenues
property recommending repairs costing $1,325.00. On the
recommendations of Mr.Cornwall it was agreed that this work
should be done.

Considerable discussion was then held regarding
the status of the Van Ness Avenue property and the investment
of the $550,000. fund, with the decision that the present
policy be continued,

No further business appearing, the meeting upon
motion adjourned.
