December 7th, 1943.

The regular quarterly meeting of the Board of Directors
of the San Francisco Ladies! Protection and Relief Society was
held at the office of Mr. N. B. Livermore, President, 216 Pine
Street, San Francisco, on Tuesday, December 7th, 1943, at 2:15 p.m.

The President, Mr. N. B. Livermore, called the meeting

to order.

Present: N. B. Livermore, Bruce Cornwall, J. S. Hutchinson,
W. P. Johnson, and E. V. Krick.

Absent: J. Cushing and M. R. Gibbons.

The minutes of the Directors meeting held on June lst,
1943 were read and approved as recorded.

The President reported that Mr. Krick had accepted the
invitation, extended to him in accordance with resolution passed
at last meeting of the Board, and was now a member of this Board.

Upon motion of Mr. Hutchinson, seconded by Judge

Johnson, it was
