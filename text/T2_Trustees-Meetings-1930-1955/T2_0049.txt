May 19th, 1942,

A special meeting of the Board of Directors of the
San Francisco Ladies' Protection and Relief Society was held at
the office of Mr. N. B. Livermore, President, 216 Pine Street,
San Francisco, on Tuesday, May 19th, 1942, at 2:15 p.m.

The President, Mr. N. B. Livermore, called the meeting
to order.

Present: WN. B. Livermore, Bruce Cornwall, J. S. Hutchinson,
W. P. Johnson, and M. R. Gibbons.

Absent: J. Cushing.

The minutes of the directors meeting held on October
28th, 1941 were read and approved as recorded.

The President reported on the activities of the Finance
Committee since last meeting of this Board, and had read a list
of the various transactions which had taken place, whereupon on
motion of Dr. Gibbons, seconded by Mr. J. S. Hutchinson, it was

RESOLVED: That the action of the Finance Committee

in making the following sales and purchases is hereby ratified,

The President further reported that the Finance Committee
had decided to place all stock certificates belonging to the
Society with the American Trust Company under the safe keeping
agreement which is at present in effect. He felt that in view
of the increasing number of stock purchases and sales, and the
danger of some certificate becoming mislaid, that this action
was advisable. The additional cost for this service by the
American Trust Company would be about $100.00 per year.

Upon motion of Dr. Gibbons, seconded by Mr. J. S.
Hutchinson, this action of the Finance Committee was unanimously

approved.
