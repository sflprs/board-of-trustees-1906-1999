August 6th, 1941.

A special meeting of the Board of Directors of the
San Francisco Ladies' Protection and Relief Society was held

in San Francisco on Wednesday, August 6th, 1941.

Present: N. B. Livermore, J. S. Hutchinson, W. P. Johnson
and J. Cushing.

Absent: Bruce Cornwall and M. R. Gibbons.
Upon motions duly made and seconded the following
resolutions were unanimously adopted:

RESOLVED: That the President or the Vice-President
of this Corporation, be and they are hereby authorized and
empowered to sell, assign, transfer and exchange, any and all
securities owned by this Society, and that any two of the follow-
ing officers, the President, the Vice-President and the Secretary
be and they are hereby authorized to endorse as the act of this
Society for any and every purpose, certificates of stock, stock
warrants and rights, registered bonds, deposit receipts for
stock and bonds, promissory notes and any and all other
documents representing interests in corporations or in real or
personal property standing in the name of this Society.

RESOLVED: That N. B. Livermore, the President, and
J. S. Hutchinson, the Secretary, be and they hereby are author-
ized in the name and behalf of this corporation to sign, seal
with the corporate seal, acknowledge and deliver the confirmatory
deed to Etha F. Smith and others of the interest of this corpor-
ation in the premises at 48-50 Pearl Street, Boston, Massachusetts,
which has just been presented to the meeting.

No further business appearing, the meeting upon motion
adjourned.
a Aeibornen,
Secretary.
