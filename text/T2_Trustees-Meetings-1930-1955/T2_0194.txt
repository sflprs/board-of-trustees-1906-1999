A general discussion of the investment portfolio
was held and the President requested Mr. Mansfield to make
a review of same and report at an early date.

The President reported that in the matter of a
survey of the Reno Property, Lerner's advise that the cost
would be $250.00, which they do not feel obligated to pay.
After discussion, the President was requested to advise
them we would not order a survey made at this time, but in
not doing so, this action abrogates no rights under the lease
in event of any misalignment of the building now on the
property.

No further business appearing, the meeting upon

motion adjourned.
