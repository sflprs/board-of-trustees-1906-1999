September 11, 1950

The regular quarterly meeting of the Board of
Directors of the San Francisco Ladies' Frotection and Relief
Society, postponed from the regular date of September 4, 1950,
was held at the office of the American Trust Company, 464
California Street, San Francisco, on wonday, September 1l,
1950, at 2:15 P.M.

The President, Mr. Moore, called the meeting to order.
Present:

Joseph moore, Jr,

John Cushing

David L. White

Barrett G. Hindes

E. V. krick

N. B. Livermore
Absent:

J. S. Hutchinson

The minutes of the regular quarterly meeting held on
June 5, 1950, were read and approved as recorded.

A discussion was held regarding the investment of
some of the cash carried in bank accounts and upon motion duly
made and seconded, the decision was made to purchase the
following securities at market:

100 Shares Union Pacific Railroad Co,

100 Shares Atchison, Topeka & Santa fe Railroad Co.

200 Shares Zastman Kodak Co,

50 Shares Insurance Company of North awerica

100 Shares Continental Insurance Co,

Decision was also made to sell the following at
market:

125 shares Potomac slectric Company

50 Shares Kansas Power & Light Co.

200 Shares Norta American Company
