Upon motion duly made and seconded it was:
RESOLVED:
That the action of the Executive Committee
in making the following transactions is
hereby ratified, approved and confirmed:
Exchanged:

Bank of California 33 Shares par value $100.
for 165 shares par value $20.

Received:
West Kentucky Coal Co- 20 shares as partial
liquidating distribution of North American

Co. on the basis of 1 share for each 10

shares held.

The President referred to the announcement that
U.S.Treasury 2% Bonds due 3-15-52 have been called for
redemption on 3-15-50. As the Society owns $25,000. of
this issue, he suggested that consideration be given to
the reinvestment of these funds at the next meeting.

Upon motion duly made and seconded it was
resolved that the Society exchange $300,000. U.S. Bonds
2% called for redemption on December 15, 1949 for a similar
amount of issue 1-3/8% due March 15, 1954 - $5,500.00 called
for redemption on same date to be added to cash fund.
