Whereas, on the Twenty-Seventh Day of December, Nineteen
Hundred and Fifty-Three, there passed from this life

NORMAN B. LIVERMORE

Whereas, Mr. Livermore had served as a member of the Board
of Directors of the San Francisco Ladies’ Protection and Relief
Society for more than thirty years and as President of such Board
for more than half of that period; and

Whereas, during all of that time he generously gave of his
time, wisdom and abilities to promote the material interests and
general welfare of this institution, thus enabling its Board of Man-
agers to maintain and extend its beneficient purposes; and

Whereas, we, his fellow Directors, in our association with
him have been ever deeply impressed by his high character, ster-
ling integrity, clear sense of fairness and unvarying courteous and
cheerful demeanor;

Row, Therefore, in recognition and appreciation of these
facts and of the irreparable loss suffered by this Society in the
passing of Mr. Livermore, be it and it is hereby

Resolved, that in respectful memory of our late President,
Norman B. Livermore, we now express and record our deep sense
of appreciation of his fine, unselfish service to this Society, and we
further express and record for the Society and ourselves profound
sorrow and regret at his passing from us; and be it further

Resolved, that this Resolution be spread in full upon the
Minutes of the Directors of the San Francisco Ladies’ Protection
and Relief Society and that a copy hereof be forwarded in expres-
sion of our deep sympathy to the family of Mr. Livermore.

DIRECTORS OF SAN FRANCISCO LADIES’
PROTECTION AND RELIEF SOCIETY

Secretary
