June 4, 1952

The regular quarterly meeting of the Board of Directors
of the San Francisco Ladies' Protection and Relief Society, was
held at the office of American Trust Company, 464 California *

Street, San Francisco, California, on Wednesday, June 4, 1952, at

2:15 P.M.

The President, Mr. Moore, called the meeting to order,
PRESENT:

Joseph A. Moore, Jr.

Bruce Cornwall

Barrett G, Hindes

David L. White

Ferard Leicester

J. S. Hutchinson
ABSENT:

John Cushing
Norman B, Livermore

The minutes of the regular quarterly meeting held on
March 4, 1952 and the special meeting held on May 7, 1952, were
read and approved as recorded.
Upon motion duly made and seconded it was:
RESOLVED:
That the action of the Executive Committee
in making the following decisions is hereby ratified,
approved and confirmed:

The increase in Liability Insurance Coverage for malpractice
from $50/100,000 to $100/500,000.

The purchase of the following securities:

Continental Illinois National Bank
and Trust Company of Chicago - 100 Shares @ 8-1/2 $8,500.00

The National City Bank of New York

200 Shares @ 47 $9,470.00
Bankers Trust Company - 200 shares @ 473 $9,570.00
$ 27,540.00

The Secretary read a letter from Mrs, E. V. Krick
acknowledging the receipt of the expression of sympathy from this
Board in the form of a copy of resolution passed by this Board
on May 7, 1952.

The President advised the Board that lirs. Krick had
resigned as President of the Society. It was agreed that the
President be requested to write Mrs, Krick and express the
regret of this Board at her resignation.
