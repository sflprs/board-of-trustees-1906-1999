Mr. West asked if we were having problems with salaries?

Miss Bridge: We are going over the salaries at present. We have
obtained comparisons from the Union, Local 250 contracts
and the Nurses Association scale. Mr. Visker at The
Heritage is compiling this data and we will make our
recommendations and bring them to you. In the past
the Board has taken action to keep our salaries as close
to union scale as possible if not above.
