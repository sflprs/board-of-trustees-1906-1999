Mr. Edwards gave an update report on the status of the ground lease
negotiations at the Cathedral Hill Hotel. He stated that the
recent real estate market deterioration, compounded by the Gulf
crisis and global problems in the financial markets, is rippling
down to Van Ness Avenue. Having held various separate meetings
with Sammon's representative, Lloyd Hanford, and the Society's
hotel consultant, Jay Scott of Laventhol & Horwath, Mr. Edwards
recommended that the ground lease not be modified unless it would
significantly enhance the Society's position. He said we should
not lock the Society into unfavorable terms in a weak real estate
market. All negotiations are now on hold while Sammons explores
other alternatives.

Mr. White thanked Mr. Edwards for his excellent negotiating skills.
