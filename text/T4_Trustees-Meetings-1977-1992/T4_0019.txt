Mr. Howard introduced Pamela Kaufmann who has supervised the
revisions of The Heritage Life Care Contract. Ms. Kaufmann stated

address past problems, to clarify the language used, to protect the

government statutes. Mr. Morshead asked if the current
cancellation policy is changed. Ms. Kaufmann stated that any old
contracts are not affected and that the new contract preserves all
of the old contract provisions. Draft #3 is the result of meetings
held between Mr. Benedict and Members of the Board of Managers and
between Mr. Benedict and Ms. Kaufmann.
