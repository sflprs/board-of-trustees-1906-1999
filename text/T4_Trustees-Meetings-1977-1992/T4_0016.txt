Executive Committee of the Board of Trustees constitutes The
Heritage Personnel Committee for the purposes of state and federal
laws as they apply to employees' civil rights. It was so affirmed.

received unanimous approval for this course of action. Mr. Howard
will send Mr. Morshead a copy of the memorandum of agreement
