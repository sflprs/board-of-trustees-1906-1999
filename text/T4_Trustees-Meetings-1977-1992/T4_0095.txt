Mr. Perkins gave the report of the Chief Financial Officer, commenting
on the highlights of the August eight month report. Revenues are

up over budget and expenses are down. Dividends are down but this
seems due to timing.

The Trustees discussed raising the rent on the Francisco Street flats.
It was moved, seconded and carried (Mr. White, Mr. Matthews) to
increase the rent of the lower flat as permitted by law as of

November 1, 1983. It was the recommendation of the Trustees that

the rent of the upper flat not be raised as the Heritage Maintenance
Supervisor occupies this flat and the raise would amount to a

decrease in his salary.
