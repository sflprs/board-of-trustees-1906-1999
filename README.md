# Board of Trustees, 1906–1999

Minutes of the Society’s Board of Trustees, which held the property and funds, 1906–1999.

Part of the photographed records of the **San Francisco Ladies’ Protection and Relief Society** (Heritage on the Marina). Browse and search every series together at **https://sflprs.github.io**.

## About each volume

- **T1 Trustees Meetings 1906–1930:** Bound ledger, begins 23 May 1906 (after the earthquake and fire). Mostly handwritten; typed minutes pasted in from the 1920s. Closes with a note that minutes continue in a new book from 29 Jan 1930.
- **T2 Trustees Meetings 1930–1955:** Spine: “Minutes Trustees 1930–1955 A”. Typed minutes, ending with the December 1955 merger and the note “This minute book is closed at this point.”
- **T3 Trustees Meetings 1969–1976:** Cover: “Board of Trustees Minutes Regular Meetings 1969–1976”.
- **T4 Trustees Meetings 1977–1992:** Spine: “Trustees – Reg. Meetings 1977–1992”. Pages were photographed from the back of the book forward.
- **T5 Trustees Meetings 1993–1999:** Wilson Jones minute book; sticky note on cover: “Mr White joined Trustees March 1, 1949” (as written).

## Folder layout

```
images/<volume>/   original photos, named <code>_<page>.jpg (e.g. T1_0001.jpg)
thumbs/<volume>/   small previews used by the viewer
text/<volume>/     machine-read (OCR) text of each page, same names, .txt
manifest.csv       one row per page: file names, original scan file, checksum, date found, notes
checksums-md5.txt  MD5 checksum of every image
```

Page numbers follow the order the photos were taken, which is not always the order of the book; some volumes were photographed newest-first or in two sessions (parts A and B in `manifest.csv`). The text is machine-read and contains errors; handwritten pages are not searchable.
